Net income (loss) attributable to common shareholders for the third quarter was ($45.1) million and earnings (loss) per share was ($3.74). These results reflect a non-cash adjustment of ($53.1) million, which decreased earnings (loss) per share by ($4.40) with respect to our investment in approximately 13.5 million shares of SAFE based on a mark-to-market at quarter end. During the third quarter, the Company sold its portfolio of municipal debt securities for $50.2 million and realized a gain of $4.5 million. The Company also sold a land parcel in Coney Island for $20.0 million and recognized a profit of $4.7 million. Additionally, the Company voluntarily paid down the outstanding balance on its margin loan facility from $94.5 million to $46.5 million.
