InspireMD Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

- Received FDA approval of CGuard® Prime for Transcarotid Arterial Revascularization (TCAR) procedures, expected to approximately double the U.S. addressable market -

 

- Company to host investor conference call today, November 12th, at 4:30pm ET -

 

Miami, FL — November 12, 2026 – InspireMD, Inc. (Nasdaq: NSPR) (“InspireMD” or the “Company”), developer of the CGuard® Prime carotid stent system for the prevention of stroke, today announced financial and operating results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Recent Business Highlights:

 

●Received U.S. Food and Drug Administration (“FDA”) approval of the CGuard® Prime TransCarotid Stent System (“CGuard Prime for TCAR”) for use in transcarotid arterial revascularization (“TCAR”) procedures, marking the return of the CGuard Prime platform to the U.S. market. The approval for the TCAR indication expands the Company’s addressable market opportunity by over 35,000 procedures annually and is supported by CGUARDIANS II pivotal trial data, which demonstrated 100% acute device success and zero death, stroke, myocardial infarction or stent thrombosis reported at 30 days. InspireMD expects to commence U.S. sales promptly in the fourth quarter of 2026.
   
●Generated continued strong international performance with revenue of $2.2 million in the third quarter of 2026, compared with $2.0 million in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of 9% year over year.
   
●Announced two upcoming scheduled talks featuring the CGuard and the CGuard Prime platforms at VEITH symposium 2026, to be held November 17-21, 2026, in New York.
   
●Successfully implemented design changes to the delivery system of the CGuard Prime Carotid Stent System (“CGuard Prime for CAS”), which is approved for use in carotid artery stenting (“CAS”) procedures. The enhanced CGuard Prime for CAS is currently undergoing final validation and performance testing in support of a premarket approval (“PMA”) supplement that the Company expects to submit to the FDA in December 2026, with potential re-launch expected during the first half of 2027.
   
●Announced the appointment of commercial leader Kathleen Kennedy as Senior Vice President of Global Sales and Marketing in advance of anticipated U.S. launch of the CGuard Prime for TCAR and the expected relaunch of CGuard Prime for CAS.
   
●Continued enrollment in the Company’s CGUARDIANS III pivotal trial of its SwitchGuard neuroprotection system, for use with its CGuard Prime for TCAR procedures.
   
●Continued to advance the original CGuard carotid stent system for CAS procedures through the FDA regulatory review process.

 

 

 

 

“FDA approval of CGuard Prime for TCAR procedures represents a transformative milestone for InspireMD, enabling the launch of our next-generation carotid implant that incorporates the latest carotid technology into a rapidly growing TCAR market,” stated Marvin Slosman, Chief Executive Officer of InspireMD. “We believe CGuard Prime for TCAR, supported by the results of our CGUARDIANS II pivotal trial, supports our mission to most effectively ‘treat the lesion’ with our advanced carotid implant, and, with its best-in-class evidence, paves the way for our return to the U.S. commercial market, strengthens our competitive position and advances our vision of establishing CGuard Prime as a leading platform for carotid intervention.”

 

“Building on this milestone, we remain focused on expanding our product portfolio and pipeline across both TCAR and CAS procedures. We continue to advance enhancements to our CGuard Prime for CAS delivery system, with submission of a PMA supplement to the FDA anticipated before year-end, while progressing our CGUARDIANS III pivotal trial evaluating our proprietary SwitchGuard neuroprotection system for TCAR procedures. We are also anticipating the introduction of a proprietary micro puncture kit, designed to support vascular access, a critical step in procedural success. Together, these initiatives reflect our commitment to building a comprehensive carotid intervention platform, with the goal of providing physicians with a broad range of solutions to address the diverse needs of patients undergoing carotid revascularization.”

 

Financial Results for the Third Quarter Ended September 30, 2026

 

For the third quarter of 2026, total revenue was $2,242,000, a decrease of $281,000, or 11.1%, compared to $2,523,000 for the third quarter of 2025.

 

International revenue was $2,210,000, an increase of $184,000, or 9.1%, compared to $2,026,000 for the third quarter of 2025. U.S. revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $32,000, compared to $497,000 for the third quarter of 2025. The U.S. sales comparison reflects the impact of the voluntary recall of the delivery system of the CGuard Prime for CAS in May 2026.

 

Gross profit (revenue less cost of revenue) for the third quarter of 2026 was $240,000, or 10.7% of revenue, compared to gross profit of $864,000, or 34.2% of revenue, for the third quarter of 2025. This decline reflects the impact of the U.S. recall action during the third quarter of 2026 and the mix within international revenue to countries with a lower average sales price. Cost of revenues was $2,002,000 compared with $1,659,000 in the prior-year period.

 

Total operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $10,193,000, a decrease of $3,722,000, or 26.7%, compared to $13,915,000 for the third quarter of 2025. Third-quarter 2026 operating expenses included $1,074,000 in restructuring expenses and related termination costs associated with the Company’s workforce reduction. Research and development expenses were $3,273,000, compared to $3,635,000, selling and marketing expenses were $2,394,000, compared to $4,392,000, and general and administrative expenses were $3,452,000, compared to $5,888,000.

 

Financial income, net, for the third quarter of 2026 was $191,000, compared to financial income, net, of $343,000 for the third quarter of 2025.

 

Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 totaled $9,762,000, or $0.12 per basic and diluted share, compared to a net loss of $12,708,000, or $0.17 per basic and diluted share, for the same period in 2025.

 

As of September 30, 2026, cash and cash equivalents and marketable securities totaled $19,246,000, compared to $54,211,000 as of December 31, 2025.

 

 

 

 

Financial Results for the Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026

 

For the first nine months of 2026, total revenue increased by $1,581,000, or 27.1%, to $7,411,000, from $5,830,000 for the same period of 2025. U.S. revenue was $859,000, compared with $551,000 in the prior-year period, while international revenue was $6,552,000, an increase of $1,273,000, or 24.1%, from $5,279,000 for the first nine months of 2025.

 

Gross profit (revenue less cost of revenue) for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $153,000, or 2.1% of revenue, compared to gross profit of $1,469,000, or 25.2% of revenue, for the same period of 2025. This decline reflects the impact of the mix of revenue in the nine months ended September 30, 2026 due to the U.S. recall action and the mix within international revenue to countries with a lower average sales price. Cost of revenue was $7,258,000 compared with $4,361,000 for the prior year period.

 

Total operating expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 were $38,529,000, a decrease of $470,000, or 1.2%, compared to $38,999,000 for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. These expenses included $1,074,000 of restructuring and other expenses in 2026. Research and development expenses were $12,331,000, compared to $11,528,000, selling and marketing expenses were $12,795,000, compared to $11,314,000, and general and administrative expenses were $12,329,000, compared to $16,157,000.

 

Financial income, net, for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $601,000, compared to $505,000 for the same period of 2025.

 

Net loss for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 totaled $37,775,000, or $0.45 per basic and diluted share, compared to a net loss of $37,025,000, or $0.64 per basic and diluted share, for the same period in 2025.
