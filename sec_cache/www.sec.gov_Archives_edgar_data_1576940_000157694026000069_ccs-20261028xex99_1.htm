Century Communities Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results

﻿

- Deliveries of 2,442 Homes Generating $864.5 Million in Total Revenues -

- Ending Community Count Increased Sequentially to 343, a Company Record -

- Book Value Per Share of $90.61, a Company Record -

- Acquired the Assets of HistoryMaker Homes on October 7, 2026 -

- Increased Capacity on Revolving Line of Credit to $1.2 Billion -

﻿

Greenwood Village, Colorado (October 28, 2026) – Century Communities, Inc. (NYSE: CCS), one of the nation’s largest homebuilders, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

﻿

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

·

Net income of $12.6 million, or $0.44 per diluted share

·

Adjusted net income of $28.8 million, or $1.01 per diluted share

·

Total revenues of $864.5 million

·

Deliveries of 2,442 homes

·

Community count of 343, a Company record

·

Book value per share of $90.61, a Company record

·

Net new home contracts of 2,284

·

GAAP homebuilding gross margin of 15.0% inclusive of inventory impairment charges

·

Adjusted homebuilding gross margin of 19.3%

·

Repurchased 169,903 shares of common stock for $11.6 million

“The third quarter was marked by a significant rise in interest rates, continued inflation and macroeconomic uncertainty, which weighed heavily on consumer confidence in general and first-time homebuyers in particular,” said Dale Francescon, Executive Chairman. “Despite these pressures, we continued to make good progress on the operational side of the business during the quarter. We grew our ending community count by 7% year-over-year and 4% sequentially to a new Company record of 343 communities. Our direct construction costs, finished lot costs, and fixed G&A excluding advertising all decreased vs. the prior quarter as we continued to effectively manage our costs and grew our book value per share to a Company record $90.61.”

Rob Francescon, Chief Executive Officer and President, said, “In early October, we acquired substantially all of the assets of HistoryMaker Homes in support of our strategy of deepening our share in existing markets to drive improved margins and returns. This acquisition will accelerate our growth in the largest housing market in the country and one we believe will benefit from solid long-term fundamentals. Specifically, the transaction will increase Century’s Dallas division’s active communities from 12 to 41 communities as of September 30, 2026 and triple deliveries from 211 to 669 homes on a trailing-twelve-month basis through the third quarter 2026.”

Acquisition of HistoryMaker Homes

Century Communities acquired substantially all of the assets of HistoryMaker Homes on October 7, 2026. Founded in 1949, HistoryMaker is Texas’ longest-standing production homebuilder and builds exclusively in the Dallas-Fort Worth metroplex. The transaction provides Century with the scale, land position and local talent to accelerate its growth in the largest housing market in the country. HistoryMaker also serves a wider range of the Dallas-Fort Worth market through its focus on both entry-level and move-up homes. Century will also maintain an ongoing land relationship with HistoryMaker's sister land development company, which will complete land development on current and future communities for Century in Dallas-Fort Worth.

 

Third Quarter 2026 Results

Net income for the third quarter 2026 was $12.6 million, or $0.44 per diluted share. Adjusted net income was $28.8 million, or $1.01 per diluted share.

﻿

Total revenues were $864.5 million, with third quarter home sales revenues totaling $842.5 million. Deliveries totaled 2,442 homes. The average sales price of home deliveries for the third quarter 2026 was $345,000.

﻿

Net new home contracts in the third quarter 2026 were 2,284, and at the end of the third quarter 2026, the Company had 1,106 homes in backlog, representing $414.0 million of backlog dollar value. Adjusted homebuilding gross margin percentage, excluding interest, purchase price accounting and inventory impairment was 19.3% in the third quarter of 2026, and homebuilding gross margin was 15.0%. Homebuilding gross margin excluding inventory impairment was 17.3%. Selling, general, and administrative expenses as a percent of home sales revenues were 14.1% in the quarter. Adjusted EBITDA and EBITDA for the third quarter 2026 were $63.4 million and $37.1 million, respectively.

﻿

Financial services revenues and pre-tax income were $21.2 million and $6.2 million, respectively, in the third quarter 2026.

﻿

Balance Sheet and Liquidity

The Company ended the third quarter 2026 with a strong financial position, including $2.6 billion of stockholders’ equity and $863.8 million of total liquidity, including $119.8 million of cash, including cash equivalents and cash held in escrow. Century amended its revolving line of credit at the end of the quarter, increasing its capacity to $1.2 billion and extending its maturity to November 2030.

﻿

Book value per share was $90.61, a Company record, as of September 30, 2026.

﻿

During the third quarter, consistent with Century’s disciplined capital allocation approach to enhance the long-term value of the Company and return capital to our stockholders, Century maintained its quarterly cash dividend of $0.32 per share and repurchased 169,903 shares of our common stock for $11.6 million.

﻿

As of September 30, 2026, homebuilding debt to capital equaled 36.8% and net homebuilding debt to net capital equaled 34.9%.

﻿

Full Year 2026 Outlook

Scott Dixon, Chief Financial Officer of the Company, commented, “Given the impact of increased mortgage rates on our orders and deliveries, we now expect our full year 2026 home deliveries to be in the range of 9,350 to 9,650 homes, including 100 deliveries from HistoryMaker in the fourth quarter, and our home sales revenues to be in the range of $3.3 billion to $3.4 billion.”

About Century Communities

Century Communities, Inc. (NYSE: CCS) is one of the nation’s largest homebuilders and a recognized industry leader in online home sales. Newsweek has named the Company one of America's Most Trustworthy Companies for three consecutive years, and Century Communities has also been designated as one of U.S. News & World Report’s Best Companies to Work For (2025-2026). Through its Century Communities and Century Complete brands, Century's mission is to build attractive, high-quality homes at affordable prices to provide its valued customers with A HOME FOR EVERY DREAM®. Century is engaged in all aspects of homebuilding — including the acquisition, entitlement and development of land, along with the construction, innovative marketing and sale of quality homes designed to appeal to a wide range of homebuyers. The Company operates in 16 states and over 45 markets across the U.S., and also offers mortgage, title, insurance brokerage, and escrow services in select markets through its Inspire Home Loans, Parkway Title, IHL Home Insurance Agency, and IHL Escrow subsidiaries. To learn more about Century Communities, please visit www.centurycommunities.com.

EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA

﻿

EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA are non-GAAP financial measures the Company uses as supplemental measures in evaluating operating performance. The Company defines EBITDA as net income before (i) income tax expense, (ii) interest in cost of home sales revenues, (iii) interest expense (income), and (iv) depreciation and amortization expense. The Company defines adjusted EBITDA as EBITDA before inventory impairment, abandonment of lot option contracts,  stock-based compensation expense, restructuring costs, loss on debt extinguishment,  impairment on other investment, and purchase price accounting for acquired work in process inventory, in each case as applicable during a period. The Company believes EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA provide an indicator of general economic performance that is not affected by fluctuations in interest rates or effective tax rates, levels of depreciation or amortization, and other specified factors that management believes affect comparability. Accordingly, the Company’s management believes that these measurements are useful for comparing general operating performance from period to period. EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA should be considered in addition to, and not as a substitute for, consolidated net income in accordance with GAAP as a measure of performance. The presentation of adjusted EBITDA should not be construed as an indication that the Company’s future results will be unaffected by unusual or other specified factors that management believes affect comparability. Each of EBITDA and adjusted EBITDA is limited as an analytical tool, and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of the Company’s results of operations as reported under GAAP.

The Company believes the ratio of net homebuilding debt to net capital is a relevant and useful financial measure to investors in understanding the leverage employed in its operations and as an indicator of the Company’s ability to obtain external financing.
