Aviat Networks Announces Fiscal 2027 First Quarter and Three Month Financial Results

Total Q1 Revenues of $102.0 million
Q1 Operating Income of $0.9 million; Q1 Non-GAAP Operating Income of $3.9 million
Q1 Net Loss of $2.8 million; Q1 Adjusted EBITDA of $6.0 million
Q1 Diluted Net Loss per Share of $0.22; Q1 Non-GAAP Diluted Earnings per Share of $0.12

AUSTIN, Texas, November 10, 2026 -- Aviat Networks, Inc. (“Aviat Networks,” “Aviat,” or the “Company”), (Nasdaq: AVNW), the leading expert in wireless transport and access solutions, today reported financial results for its fiscal 2027 first quarter ended October 2, 2026.

First Quarter Highlights
•Record first-quarter bookings of over $150 million, up 75% year-over-year resulting in a quarterly book-to bill ratio greater than 1.5
•Aprisa bookings grew 61% compared to the year-ago period, signaling growing momentum with North American utility companies
•Ongoing cost controls lowered Non-GAAP operating expenses to $26.2 million, strengthening profitability in the quarter

First Quarter QTD Financial Highlights

•Total Revenues: $102.0 million
•GAAP Results: Gross Margin 29.6%; Operating Expenses $29.3 million; Operating Income $0.9 million; Net Loss $2.8 million; Net Loss per diluted share (“Net Loss per share”) $0.22
•Non-GAAP Results: Adjusted EBITDA $6.0 million; Gross Margin 29.6%; Operating Expenses $26.2 million; Operating Income $3.9 million; Net Income $1.5 million; Net Income per share $0.12
•Cash and cash equivalents: $55.4 million
•Net debt: $30.5 million

Fiscal 2027 First Quarter and Three Months Ended October 2, 2026
Revenues
The Company reported total revenues of $102.0 million for its fiscal 2027 first quarter, compared to $107.3 million in the fiscal 2026 first quarter, a decrease of approximately $5.4 million or 5.0%. North America revenue of $49.2 million decreased by $3.4 million or 6.5%, compared to $52.6 million in the prior year due to timing of certain private and mobile network projects. International revenue of $52.7 million decreased by approximately $1.9 million or 3.5%, compared to $54.7 million in the prior year, primarily due to timing of capital expenditure plans of mobile network operators.
Gross Margins
In the fiscal 2027 first quarter, the Company reported GAAP gross margin of 29.6% and non-GAAP gross margin of 29.6%. This compares to GAAP gross margin of 33.2% and non-GAAP gross margin of 33.8% in the fiscal 2026 first quarter, a decrease of 360 and 420 basis points, respectively. The decrease was driven by regional and product mix in the quarter.
Operating Expenses
The Company reported GAAP total operating expenses of $29.3 million for the fiscal 2027 first quarter, compared to $30.5 million in the fiscal 2026 first quarter. Non-GAAP total operating expenses, excluding the impact of restructuring charges, share-based compensation, and litigation and other expenses for the fiscal 2027 first quarter were $26.2 million, compared to $28.4 million in the prior year, a decrease of $2.1 million or 7.5%.
Operating Income
The Company reported GAAP operating income of $0.9 million for the fiscal 2027 first quarter, compared to GAAP operating income of $5.2 million in the fiscal 2026 first quarter, a decrease of $4.3 million. Operating income decreased primarily due to lower gross margin dollars. On a non-GAAP basis, the Company reported operating income of $3.9 million for the fiscal 2027 first quarter, compared to non-GAAP operating income of $7.9 million in the prior year, a decrease of $4.0 million.
Net Loss / Income / Net Loss / Income Per Share
The Company reported GAAP net loss of $2.8 million in the fiscal 2027 first quarter or GAAP net loss per share of $0.22. This compared to GAAP net income of $0.2 million or GAAP net income per share of $0.01 in the fiscal 2026 first quarter. On a non-GAAP basis, the Company reported non-GAAP net income of $1.5 million or non-GAAP net income per share of $0.12, compared to non-GAAP net income of $5.5 million or $0.43 per share in the prior year.
Adjusted EBITDA
Adjusted earnings before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization (“Adjusted EBITDA”) for the fiscal 2027 first quarter was $6.0 million, compared to $9.1 million in the fiscal 2026 first quarter.
Balance Sheet Highlights
The Company reported $55.4 million in cash and cash equivalents as of October 2, 2026, compared to $72.8 million as of July 3, 2026. As of October 2, 2026, total debt was $85.9 million, a decrease of $11.1 million from July 3, 2026.

Fiscal 2027 Full Year Outlook
The Company is updating its fiscal 2027 full year guidance of:
•Full year Revenue between $450 and $475 million
•Full year Adjusted EBITDA between $50.0 and $55.0 million

About Aviat Networks
Aviat Networks, Inc. is the leading expert in wireless transport and access solutions and works to provide dependable products, services and support to its customers. With more than one million systems sold into 170 countries worldwide, communications service providers and private network operators including state/local government, utility, federal government and defense organizations trust Aviat with their critical applications. Coupled with a long history of microwave innovations, Aviat provides a comprehensive suite of localized professional and support services enabling customers to drastically simplify both their networks and their lives. For more than 70 years, the experts at Aviat have delivered high performance products, simplified operations, and the best overall customer experience. Aviat is headquartered in Austin, Texas. For more information, visit www.aviatnetworks.com or connect with Aviat Networks on Facebook and LinkedIn.

RECONCILIATION OF NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES AND REGULATION G DISCLOSURE
To supplement the consolidated financial statements presented in accordance with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (GAAP), we provide additional measures of gross margin, research and development expenses, selling and administrative expenses, operating expenses, operating income, provision for or benefit from income taxes, net income, net income per share, and adjusted income before interest, tax, depreciation and amortization (Adjusted EBITDA), in each case, adjusted to exclude certain costs, charges, gains and losses, as set forth below. We believe that these non-GAAP financial measures, when considered together with the GAAP financial measures provide information that is useful to investors in understanding period-over-period operating results separate and apart from items that may, or could, have a disproportionate positive or negative impact on results in any particular period. We also believe these non-GAAP measures enhance the ability of investors to analyze trends in our business and to understand our performance. In addition, we may utilize non-GAAP financial measures as a guide in our forecasting, budgeting and long-term planning process and to measure operating performance for some management compensation purposes. Any analysis of non-GAAP financial measures should be used only in conjunction with results presented in accordance with GAAP. Reconciliations of these non-GAAP financial measures with the most directly comparable financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP follow.
