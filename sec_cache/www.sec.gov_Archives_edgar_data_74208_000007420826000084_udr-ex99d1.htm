Capital Allocation Activity

During and subsequent to quarter-end, the Company,

●Closed the previously announced $2.0 billion strategic partnership with Apollo by contributing 19 apartment communities totaling 7,059 apartment homes valued at an approximate 6.0 percent capitalization rate to a new joint venture. Under the terms of the transaction, UDR received $1.34 billion of equity capital from Apollo, retained an approximate one-third interest in the joint venture, will continue to operate and manage the portfolio for which it will earn an asset management fee, and retained an option to repurchase Apollo’s interest in the joint venture at a 7.0 percent target IRR for a finite period starting at the fifth anniversary of closing.
●Through the end of October, repurchased approximately 5.2 million shares of its common stock at a weighted average share price of $35.48 for total consideration of approximately $185.1 million. Following this share repurchase activity, the Company has approximately 20.3 million shares remaining for repurchase under its program. Since recommencing share repurchases in September 2025, the Company has repurchased approximately 16.7 million shares of its common stock at a weighted average share price of $36.06 for total consideration of approximately $603.2 million.
●Sold two apartment communities, one each in Seattle and Nashville, with a combined 412 apartment homes for gross proceeds of $183.0 million. The Company remains under contract to sell a 396-home apartment community in the Metropolitan Washington, D.C. area for gross proceeds of approximately $69.0 million.
●Expanded its joint venture with LaSalle Investment Management to approximately $958.3 million by contributing a 360-home apartment community in Nashville, TN, to the venture while simultaneously placing debt on the newly contributed community and an existing community in the venture. Under the terms of the transaction, the Company received approximately $103.2 million in cash proceeds.
●Continued to execute on the runoff of its Debt and Preferred Equity (“DPE”) portfolio, as announced last quarter, via the liquidation of the Company’s interest in various DPE joint ventures. As previously disclosed, the Company acquired a 286-home community in Portland, OR, for an incremental cash outlay of $74.7 million based on a property valuation of $104.3 million. During the quarter, the Company also reached a resolution with a developer partner on a two-property portfolio located in California’s Inland Empire. This resulted in the acquisition of a 483-home community during the quarter for an incremental cash outlay of $160.3 million based on a property valuation of $250.1 million as well as an agreement to acquire a 237-home community in the first half of 2027, subject to customary closing conditions. Excluding the latter Inland Empire transaction, the aggregate investment balance of the Company’s Debt and Preferred Equity portfolio is expected to be approximately $250.0 million at year-end 2026.
●Received a full repayment of approximately $29.5 million on its Preferred Equity investment in a stabilized apartment community in Orlando, FL.

Operating Results

In the third quarter, total revenue decreased by $3.8 million YOY, or (0.9) percent, as revenue increases attributable to growth from Same-Store and acquired communities were offset by the removal of revenue from properties that were sold.

“Third quarter Same-Store results were supported by strong occupancy in the mid-96 percent range, year-over-year improvement in bad debt, blended lease rate growth that fell within our expected range, and continued improvement in resident retention that led to strong expense control,” said Mike Lacy, UDR’s Chief Operating Officer. “Operating from a position of occupancy strength, with fewer lease expirations than typical in the fourth quarter following a strategic shift to the expiration schedule made last year, and renewal lease offers higher than those in the third quarter, we remain in an attractive position to drive revenue and NOI growth as we conclude a successful year.”

Balance Sheet Update

The Company’s total indebtedness as of September 30, 2026, was $5.8 billion at a weighted average interest rate of 3.45 percent, with $333.6 million, or 6.6 percent of total consolidated debt, maturing through 2027, including principal amortization and excluding amounts on the Company’s line of credit, commercial paper program, and working capital credit facility. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had approximately $641.6 million in liquidity through a combination of cash and undrawn capacity on its credit facilities. Immediately following the closing of the Apollo transaction, the Company received $1.34 billion of equity proceeds. Please see Attachment 13 of the Company’s related quarterly Supplement for additional details regarding investment guidance.

About UDR, Inc.

UDR, Inc. (NYSE: UDR), an S&P 500 company, is a leading multifamily real estate investment trust with a demonstrated performance history of delivering superior and dependable returns by successfully managing, buying, selling, developing and redeveloping attractive real estate communities in targeted U.S. markets. As of September 30, 2026, UDR owned or had an ownership position in 60,617 apartment homes, including 634 apartment homes under development. For over 54 years, UDR has delivered long-term value to shareholders, the best standard of service to Residents, and the highest quality experience for Associates.
