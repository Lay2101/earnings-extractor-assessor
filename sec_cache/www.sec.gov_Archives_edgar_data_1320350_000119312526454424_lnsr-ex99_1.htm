LENSAR® Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Provides Business Update

 

10 ALLY Robotic Cataract Laser Systems® (“ALLY System”) Placements in Third Quarter 2026; Backlog of 12 ALLY Systems as of September 30, 2026

 

Third Quarter Recurring Revenue Grew 17% Year Over Year to $12.6 Million

 

Total Laser Installed Base Reaches 450 Systems; ALLY System Installed Base Expands 22% Year Over Year

ORLANDO, Fla. (Nov 10, 2026) - LENSAR, Inc. (Nasdaq: LNSR) (“LENSAR” or the “Company”), a global medical technology company focused on advanced robotic laser solutions for the treatment of cataracts, today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 and provided an update on key operational initiatives.

“In-line with the expectations we shared in August, our third quarter results reflected the typical summer seasonality during which surgical scheduling and purchasing decisions contribute to a more measured pace of activity, leading to a sequential decline from a very strong second quarter,” said Nick Curtis, President and CEO of LENSAR. “Total revenue declined 1% year-over-year due to lower system sales against a robust number of sold systems in the prior year period, which was partially offset by growth in both procedure volume and recurring revenue. We see the year-over-year growth in these two revenue indicators as clear evidence of the increasing strength of our operational foundation, which we plan to continue leveraging to drive sustainable growth over the long-term.”

“LENSAR was built on innovation, and that remains a fundamental aspect of how we intend to continue competing and gain further ground in the laser cataract surgery market,” continued Curtis. “Over the quarter, our ALLY installed base grew to approximately 225 systems, expanding our opportunity to support surgeons and generate recurring revenue through continued growth in procedure volumes. We remain focused on advancing the capabilities and adoption of our technology, deepening relationships with surgeon partners and distributors, and utilizing this growing installed base to drive success. While quarterly results may fluctuate due to the timing of system sales and placements, among other factors, our commitment to innovation and disciplined commercial execution remains unchanged.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

During the three months ended September 30, 2026, total revenue was $14.1 million, compared with $14.3 million in the prior year period. As disclosed in the Company’s second quarter operational update, the third quarter is typically the slowest of the year for cataract procedures and sales activity given extended holidays in various parts of the world and

 

 

 

 

 

summer vacations in the United States. Procedure volume was 52,545 in the third quarter, compared with 46,811 in the third quarter of 2025. Recurring revenue increased 17% year over year to $12.6 million and represented 89% of total revenue, compared with $10.8 million, or 75% of total revenue a year earlier.

The Company placed 10 ALLY Systems during the third quarter, bringing the total installed ALLY base to approximately 225 ALLY Systems at quarter end. The total installed base reached approximately 450 systems. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had a backlog of 12 ALLY Systems pending installation. The Company continues to prioritize differentiated technology and broader adoption of the ALLY System as it works to strengthen its recurring revenue base over time.

About LENSAR

 

LENSAR is a commercial-stage medical device company focused on designing, developing, and marketing advanced systems for the treatment of cataracts and the management of astigmatism as an integral aspect of the procedure. LENSAR has developed its ALLY Robotic Cataract Laser System® as a compact, highly ergonomic system utilizing an extremely fast dual-modality laser and proprietary imaging and software. ALLY is designed to transform premium cataract surgery by utilizing LENSAR’s advanced robotic technologies with the ability to perform the entire procedure in a sterile operating room or in-office surgical suite, delivering operational efficiencies and reduced overhead. ALLY includes LENSAR’s proprietary Streamline® software technology, designed to guide surgeons to achieve better outcomes.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures: The Company prepares and analyzes operating and financial data and non-GAAP measures to assess the performance of its business, make strategic and offering decisions and build its financial projections. The key non-GAAP measures it uses are EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA. EBITDA is defined as net (loss) income before interest expense, interest income, income tax expense, depreciation and amortization expenses. EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure. EBITDA is included in this filing because we believe that EBITDA provides meaningful supplemental information for investors regarding the performance of our business and facilitates a meaningful evaluation of actual results on a comparable basis with historical results. Adjusted EBITDA is also a non-GAAP financial measure. We believe Adjusted EBITDA, which is defined as EBITDA and further excluding stock-based compensation expense, change in fair value of warrant liabilities, acquisition-related income and costs, and strategic transaction-related costs provides meaningful supplemental information for investors when evaluating our results and comparing us to peer companies as stock-based compensation expense and change in fair value of warrant liabilities are significant non-cash charges, and acquisition-related income and costs and strategic transaction-related costs are not part of our core operating performance. We use these non-GAAP financial measures in order to have comparable financial results to analyze changes in our underlying business from quarter to quarter. However, there are a number of limitations related to the use of non-GAAP measures and their nearest GAAP equivalents. For example, other companies may calculate non-GAAP measures differently, or may use other measures to calculate their financial performance and, therefore, any non-GAAP measures we use may not be directly comparable to similarly titled measures of other companies. Investors should not consider our non-GAAP financial measures in isolation or as a substitute for an analysis of our results as reported under GAAP.
