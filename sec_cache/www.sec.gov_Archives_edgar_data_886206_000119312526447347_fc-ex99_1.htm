FRANKLIN COVEY REPORTS FINANCIAL RESULTS FOR

FOURTH QUARTER AND FULL FISCAL 2026

 

Consolidated Fourth Quarter Revenue of $70.0 Million and Fiscal 2026 Revenue of

$261.5 Million within Updated Guidance Range

 

Net Income for the Fourth Quarter Increases to $7.1 Million

 

Fourth Quarter Adjusted EBITDA Increases 9% to $12.8 Million and Fiscal 2026

Adjusted EBITDA of $28.9 Million within Original Full-Year Guidance Range

 

Deferred Revenue Increases 4% Year-over-Year to $116.1 Million; Enterprise Division

Deferred Revenue Increases 14% to $64.9 Million

 

Liquidity Remains Strong at Over $75 Million, with $13.3 Million of Cash and

the Company’s $62.5 Million Credit Facility Fully Available

 

Company Provides Fiscal 2027 Guidance

 

 

Salt Lake City, Utah – Franklin Covey Co. (NYSE: FC), a global leadership and organizational performance partner that gives strategy the human edge, announced today its financial results for the fourth quarter and fiscal year ended August 31, 2026.

 

Fiscal 2026 results reflect growing traction from the Company’s investments in its Enterprise North America go-to-market model, where full-year invoiced amounts increased 4% and deferred revenue increased 17%, supporting the Company’s outlook for revenue and Adjusted EBITDA growth in fiscal 2027.

 

Fourth Quarter Fiscal 2026 Financial Overview

 

The Company’s consolidated revenue for Q4 FY2026 was within its updated guidance range at $70.0 million compared with $71.2 million in Q4 FY2025. The Company’s financial results for Q4 FY2026 include the following:

 

o
Enterprise Division revenue for Q4 FY2026 totaled $45.5 million compared with $45.7 million in the prior year.
▪
Enterprise Division revenue reflected a $0.1 million decrease in North America segment revenue and a $0.1 million decrease in International segment revenue.
▪
Enterprise North America invoiced amounts totaled $37.0 million, essentially flat compared with $37.2 million in Q4 FY2025.
▪
Deferred revenue for the Enterprise Division increased 14% to $64.9 million compared with $57.1 million at August 31, 2025. Deferred revenue for Enterprise North America increased 17% to $59.2 million compared with $50.4 million at August 31, 2025.
o
Education Division revenue in Q4 FY2026 totaled $23.6 million compared with $24.4 million in the prior year.
o
Subscription and contractually committed services invoiced for Q4 FY2026 totaled $62.8 million compared with $61.4 million in Q4 FY2025.
o
The Company recognized net income for Q4 FY2026 of $7.1 million, or $0.62 per diluted share, compared with net income of $4.4 million, or $0.34 per diluted share, in Q4 FY2025.
o
Adjusted EBITDA for Q4 FY2026 was within Company guidance and increased 9% to $12.8 million compared with $11.7 million in the prior year.

 

 

o
Consolidated deferred revenue at August 31, 2026 increased 4% to $116.1 million compared with $111.7 million at August 31, 2025.
o
Cash provided by operating activities for Q4 FY2026 was $4.1 million compared with $9.9 million in the prior year.
▪
Free cash flow for Q4 FY2026 was $0.7 million compared with $1.5 million in Q4 FY2025.
▪
Cash and cash equivalents totaled $13.3 million compared with $31.7 million as of August 31, 2025.

 

Fiscal 2026 Financial Overview

 

The Company’s consolidated revenue for FY2026 was within updated guidance at $261.5 million compared with $267.1 million in FY2025. The Company’s financial results for FY2026 include the following:

 

o
Enterprise Division revenue for FY2026 totaled $182.7 million compared with $188.1 million in the prior year.
▪
Enterprise Division revenue performance was impacted by a $5.0 million decrease in North America segment revenue and a $0.4 million decrease in International segment revenue.
▪
Enterprise North America invoiced amounts for FY2026 increased 4% to $151.4 million compared with $144.9 million in FY2025.
o
Education Division revenue in FY2026 increased to $76.2 million compared with $74.6 million in the prior year.
o
Subscription and contractually committed services invoiced for FY2026 totaled $165.1 million compared with $151.7 million in FY2025.
o
The Company recognized net income for FY2026 of $4.9 million, or $0.42 per diluted share, compared with net income of $3.1 million, or $0.24 per diluted share, in FY2025.
o
Adjusted EBITDA for FY2026 was within Company guidance and totaled $28.9 million compared with $28.8 million in the prior year.
o
Cash provided by operating activities for FY2026 was $21.6 million compared with $29.0 million in the prior year.
▪
Free cash flow for FY2026 was $9.2 million compared with $12.1 million in the prior year.
o
The Company purchased approximately 1,573,000 shares of its common stock for a total of $28.1 million during FY2026, including approximately 1,504,000 shares purchased in the open market under 10b5-1 trading plans and approximately 69,000 shares withheld to cover statutory income taxes on stock-based compensation awards that vested during the year.

 

Paul Walker, President and Chief Executive Officer, commented, “In fiscal 2026, the investments we made to build a stronger, faster-growing company gained traction and delivered measurable results. In Enterprise North America, our largest business and the primary focus of these investments, invoiced amounts grew 4% to $151.4 million, and deferred revenue grew 17% to $59.2 million. These increases reflect growing client commitments and give us greater visibility into revenue we expect to recognize in fiscal 2027.

 

“Our go-to-market model is working, and we are seeing increasingly clear evidence of its potential. Compared with fiscal 2024, the year before we launched the new model, invoiced amounts per new-client sales professional have more than doubled, and existing clients are expanding their work with us. Our Education business remains strong, with school retention at its highest level since 2022 and Leader in Me now serving more than 8,000 schools worldwide. With larger client commitments and a more productive sales engine, we believe we are well positioned to deliver renewed revenue growth and improved profitability in fiscal 2027 and beyond.”

 

Jessi Betjemann, Chief Financial Officer, said, “We delivered fourth-quarter and fiscal 2026 revenue within our updated guidance range and full-year Adjusted EBITDA within both our original and narrowed guidance ranges, while strengthening our cost structure and continuing to invest in growth. We grew fourth-quarter Adjusted EBITDA 9% despite lower revenue, reflecting the operating discipline we have built into our business. As a larger base of contracted commitments translates into reported revenue, we expect to benefit from operating leverage, with fiscal 2027 Adjusted EBITDA growing faster than revenue.”

 

Fiscal 2027 Guidance

 

The Company expects revenue and Adjusted EBITDA to grow in FY2027, supported by the growing volume of client commitments, increased invoiced amounts in Enterprise North America, and services bookings generated during FY2026. As this activity converts into reported revenue over time, the Company expects improved profitability.

Non-GAAP Financial Information

 

This earnings release includes the concepts of Adjusted EBITDA and Free Cash Flow which are non-GAAP measures. The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as net income or loss excluding the impact of interest, income taxes, intangible asset amortization, depreciation, stock-based compensation expense, and certain other infrequently occurring items such as restructuring and building exit costs. Free Cash Flow is defined as GAAP calculated cash flows from operating activities less capitalized expenditures for purchases of property and equipment, curriculum development, and content or license rights. The Company references these non-GAAP financial measures in its decision-making because they provide supplemental information that facilitates consistent internal comparisons to the historical operating performance of prior periods, and the Company believes they provide investors with greater transparency to evaluate operational activities and financial results. Refer to the attached tables for the reconciliation of the non-GAAP financial measure, Adjusted EBITDA, to consolidated net income, a related GAAP financial measure, and for the calculation of Free Cash Flow.

 

The Company is unable to provide a reconciliation of the above forward-looking estimate of non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA to GAAP measures because certain information needed to make a reasonable forward-looking estimate is difficult to obtain and dependent on future events which may be uncertain, or out of the Company’s control, including the amount of AAP contracts invoiced, the number of AAP contracts that are renewed, necessary costs to deliver the Company’s offerings, such as unanticipated curriculum development costs, and other potential variables. Accordingly, a reconciliation is not available without unreasonable effort.
