PEAPACK-GLADSTONE FINANCIAL CORPORATION

REPORTS THIRD QUARTER FINANCIAL RESULTS

 

Bedminster, N.J. – October 27, 2026 – Peapack-Gladstone Financial Corporation (NASDAQ Global Select Market: PGC) (the "Company") announced its third quarter 2026 financial results.

This earnings release should be read in conjunction with the Company’s Q3 2026 Investor Update, a copy of which is available on our website at www.peapackprivate.com and via a Current Report on Form 8-K on the website of the Securities and Exchange Commission at www.sec.gov.

 

The Company reported third quarter 2026 financial results that reflect continued execution of its private banking strategy and earnings momentum. Total revenue increased 24% year-over-year to $87.5 million, representing the Company's tenth consecutive quarter of revenue growth, as net interest income increased 33% to $67.5 million and net interest margin expanded to 3.34%. Wealth management fee income of $17.3 million represented 20% of total revenue. At the same time, operating expenses increased only 5% year-over-year and declined on a linked-quarter basis, producing another quarter of meaningful positive operating leverage. These results reflect the maturation of the Company's strategic investments across the Metropolitan New York market and reinforce management's confidence in the long-term earnings power of the franchise. The Company continued to generate relationship-based balance sheet growth, with total loans reaching $7.1 billion and deposits reaching $7.4 billion at September 30, 2026.

 

Douglas L. Kennedy, President and CEO stated, “Our investments in experienced teams across New York City, Westchester and Long Island are translating into deeper client relationships and strong loan and deposit growth. We have built the capabilities and geographic presence to serve more of our clients’ financial needs, and the earnings contribution from that platform continues to grow."

Mr. Kennedy added, "We enter the fourth quarter with strong pipelines and confidence in our teams. Our focus is on increasing productivity across the platform we have built, maintaining pricing and expense discipline, and translating continued relationship growth into higher returns. Credit and capital discipline remain central to that execution.”

Our third quarter results reflect continued momentum and sustainability in delivering enhanced shareholder value. The continued growth in net interest income and expansion in net interest margin, together with disciplined expense management, led to net income available to common shareholders of $18.5 million, or $0.99 per diluted share for the third quarter, compared to $15.8 million, or $0.86 per diluted share, for the linked quarter and $9.6 million, or $0.54 per diluted share for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Net income available to common shareholders increased 17% on a linked-quarter basis, and diluted earnings per share increased 83% year-over-year. Operating expenses declined 1% sequentially to $55.1 million; the GAAP efficiency ratio improved 166 basis points to 63.03%. Asset quality also improved, with nonperforming assets declining 20% sequentially to $55.8 million, or 0.68% of total assets. Tangible book value per share increased 6% year-over-year to $36.24 at September 30, 2026.

During the first quarter the Company announced a commitment by Strategic Value Bank Partners to purchase up to $50 million of convertible preferred stock. Strategic Value Bank Partners is a well-known, long-term investor primarily focused on the banking sector. The commitment included an initial $30 million private placement of the preferred stock which closed during March 2026 with the ability to issue an additional $20 million through the end of 2027. Based on this quarter’s results and projected growth rates, we elected to issue the remaining $20 million of our 6% non-cumulative perpetual convertible preferred stock on July 24, 2026.

Earnings and Operating Leverage

 

The Company generated strong revenue growth of 24% year-over-year, with total revenue of $87.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $86.1 million for the second quarter of 2026 and $70.7 million for the third quarter of 2025. Revenue growth was primarily attributable to the consistent improvement in net interest income over the last twelve months. The increase in revenue and continued expense discipline translated into higher earnings driving positive operating leverage and improved profitability.

Operating expenses decreased to $55.1 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $55.7 million for the second quarter of 2026. Operating expenses were $52.3 million for the third quarter of 2025. The GAAP efficiency ratio improved for an eighth consecutive quarter to approximately 63%.

 

Net Interest Income and Margin

 

Net interest income totaled $67.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, an increase of $3.6 million, or 6%, from the second quarter of 2026 and an increase of $16.9 million, or 33%, from the third quarter of 2025. Net interest margin expanded to 3.34% compared to 3.32% in the prior quarter and 2.81% in the third quarter of 2025, continuing the upward trend over the past several quarters. This improvement in net interest income and net interest margin was primarily driven by balance sheet repositioning, disciplined pricing and improved earning-asset yields.

 

Loans / Commercial Banking

Total loans increased $403 million, or 24% on an annualized basis, to $7.1 billion at September 30, 2026, compared to $6.7 billion at June 30, 2026. Loans have grown $1.1 billion year-over-year. Growth remained concentrated in our core areas of strength, particularly C&I and commercial real estate. Commercial mortgage activity was bolstered by sponsor demand for stabilized assets and refinancing activity. C&I growth was driven by business

2

expansion and capital investment. Total C&I loans and leases at September 30, 2026 were $3.1 billion, or 43% of the total loan portfolio.

Mr. Kennedy noted, “The strength of our loan growth is measured not only by its pace, but also by its quality. Our bankers continue to onboard core relationships, delivering a coordinated approach that integrates commercial banking, personal banking, treasury management and wealth management through a single point of contact. This relationship model deepens client engagement and enables us to compete effectively with much larger institutions."

 

Wealth Management

 

John Babcock, President of the Bank’s Wealth Management Division, stated, “Wealth Management generated $346 million of gross client inflows during the quarter, reflecting the strength of our client relationships and the breadth of our advisory platform. We continue to deepen those relationships by bringing investment management, trust, tax, financial planning and other specialized services together with the Bank’s lending and liquidity capabilities to serve clients and their families over time.”

 

Funding / Liquidity / Interest Rate Risk Management

 

Total deposits increased $301 million, or 17% annualized, to $7.4 billion at September 30, 2026, from $7.1 billion at June 30, 2026. Relationship-based deposits have created solid franchise value for our Company. Noninterest-bearing deposits increased $232 million year-over-year, representing 29% of total deposit growth over the last twelve months and a meaningful component of total funding, supporting both margin expansion and balance sheet stability. In addition, short-term borrowings increased $206 million during the third quarter of 2026 to supplement deposit funding and support continued loan growth, while maintaining appropriate liquidity levels.

The Company’s liquidity profile remains strong with a loan-to-deposit ratio of 96%. At September 30, 2026, the Company’s balance sheet liquidity totaled $1.1 billion, or 13% of total assets. The Company maintains additional liquidity resources of approximately $4.2 billion through secured available borrowing facilities with the Federal Home Loan Bank and the Federal Reserve Discount Window. The available funding from the Federal Home Loan Bank and the Federal Reserve are secured by the Company’s loan and investment portfolios. The Company's total on and off-balance sheet liquidity totaled $5.3 billion at September 30, 2026, which amounted to 214% of the total uninsured/uncollateralized deposits currently on the Company's balance sheet. The Company continues to maintain a well-diversified funding base with a high level of operating deposits and no reliance on brokered funding.

 

Income Taxes

 

The effective tax rate was 20.5% for the September 2026 quarter, compared to 28.4% for the June 2026 quarter and 29.2% for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The effective tax rate for the third quarter of 2026 included a discrete income tax benefit of approximately $1.8 million related to the purchase of transferable production tax credits.

 

Asset Quality / Provision for Credit Losses

Nonperforming assets decreased to $57.8 million, or 0.68% of total assets compared to $72.2 million, or 0.91% of total assets, at June 30, 2026. The decrease in nonperforming assets during the third quarter of 2026 reflects the Company's concentrated effort to resolve problem loans. Loans past due 30 through 89 days and still accruing decreased to $39.4 million, or 0.56% of total loans at September 30, 2026, compared to $48.1 million, or 0.72% of total loans, at June 30, 2026.

 

Mr. Kennedy noted, “During the quarter, we made progress in reducing nonperforming assets and delinquent loan balances, while loans subject to special mention and classified also declined. These trends reflect our active management of individual credit situations and the progress we are making in resolving them. We remain focused on disciplined underwriting, proactive portfolio oversight and maintaining reserve coverage that is appropriate for the risk profile of the portfolio."

The provision for credit losses totaled $8.5 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $8.1 million for the second quarter of 2026 and $4.8 million for the September 30, 2025 quarter. During the third quarter of 2026, approximately $5.0 million of the provision was attributable to loan growth of $403 million while $4.6 million related to increases in specific reserves, partially offset by improved economic factors.

3

At September 30, 2026, the allowance for credit losses ("ACL") was $73.0 million (1.03% of total loans), compared to $69.2 million (1.04% of total loans) at June 30, 2026. The increase in the ACL was due to the provision for credit losses of $8.5 million, partially offset by net charge-offs of $4.7 million. Charge-offs consisted of $1.7 million during the period related to two multifamily properties and $2.8 million related to seven commercial and industrial loans. These charge-offs were partially offset by recoveries of $25,000 during the third quarter of 2026.

Capital

The Company’s capital position remained solid during the third quarter of 2026 and continued to benefit from earnings generation. Based on our continued growth, we elected in July to issue the remaining $20 million of our 6% non-cumulative perpetual convertible preferred stock available under the $50 million commitment announced in the first quarter. Based on current results, projected growth and capital needs, management determined that completing the remaining issuance was appropriate to support continued relationship-based balance sheet growth, while maintaining prudent capital levels.

To further support capital, the Company has reinstated the feature in our Dividend Reinvestment Plan which will allow participants to purchase shares of our common stock through the Plan at a 3% discount to the current market price. This enhancement will become effective beginning with the Company's next dividend payment in November 2026.

Tangible book value per share increased 6% to $36.24 per share at September 30, 2026 from $34.10 at September 30, 2025. Book value per share increased 6% to $38.67 per share at September 30, 2026 compared to $36.62 at September 30, 2025.

As of September 30, 2026, the Company and the Bank maintained strong regulatory capital ratios. The Tier 1 Leverage Ratio at September 30, 2026 was 9.13% for the Bank and 9.29% for the Company, while the Common Equity Tier 1 Ratio was 10.59% for the Bank and 10.06% for the Company. Where applicable, such ratios remain well above regulatory well capitalized standards.

ABOUT THE COMPANY

Founded in 1921, Peapack Private Bank & Trust is a boutique private bank serving successful individuals, families, business owners, family offices, and their trusted advisors through personalized relationships and bespoke banking, lending, wealth management, investment banking, and trust solutions. PEAPACK-GLADSTONE FINANCIAL CORPORATION

NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES RECONCILIATION

Tangible book value per share and tangible common equity as a percentage of tangible assets at period end are non-GAAP financial measures derived from GAAP-based amounts. We calculate tangible common equity by subtracting goodwill, other intangible assets and preferred stock from total shareholders’ equity. Tangible assets are calculated by subtracting goodwill, and other intangible assets from total assets. We calculate tangible book value per share by dividing tangible common equity by common shares outstanding, as compared to book value per common share, which we calculate by dividing total common equity by common shares outstanding at period end. We calculate tangible common equity as a percentage of tangible assets at period end by dividing tangible common equity by tangible assets at period end. Management believes these non-GAAP measures are useful to investors in assessing the amount of capital attributable to common shareholders and facilitate comparisons with other banking organizations that use similar measures.

The efficiency ratio is a non-GAAP measure of expense control relative to recurring revenue. We calculate the efficiency ratio by dividing total noninterest expenses, excluding other real estate owned provision, as determined under GAAP, by net interest income and total noninterest income as determined under GAAP, but excluding net gains/(losses) on loans held for sale at lower of cost or fair value and excluding net gains on securities from this calculation, which we refer to below as recurring revenue. We believe that this provides a reasonable measure of core expenses relative to core revenue.

We believe these non-GAAP financial measures provide information that is important to investors and useful in understanding our financial position, results and ratios because our management internally assesses our performance based, in part, on these measures. However, these non-GAAP financial measures are supplemental and are not a substitute for an analysis based on GAAP measures. As other companies may use different calculations for these measures, this presentation may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures reported by other companies. A reconciliation of the non-GAAP measures of tangible common equity, tangible book value per share, return on average tangible common equity, and efficiency ratio to the underlying GAAP numbers is set forth below.
