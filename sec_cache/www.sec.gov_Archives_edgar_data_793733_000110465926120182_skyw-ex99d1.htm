SkyWest, Inc. Announces Third Quarter 2026 Profit

​

●Q3 2026 pre-tax income of $159 million, up from $157 million in Q3 2025 and up sequentially from $139 million in Q2 2026
●Q3 2026 diluted earnings per share of $3.08, up from $2.81 in Q3 2025 and up sequentially from $2.54 in Q2 2026
●SkyWest took delivery of four new E175 aircraft in Q3 2026 under a previously announced agreement with United Airlines

ST. GEORGE, UTAH, October 29, 2026 -- SkyWest, Inc. (NASDAQ: SKYW) (“SkyWest”) today reported financial and operating results for Q3 2026, including net income of $120 million, or $3.08 per diluted share, compared to net income of $116 million, or $2.81 per diluted share, for Q3 2025. The Q3 2026 financial results were negatively impacted by higher fuel cost per gallon in SkyWest’s prorate business compared to Q3 2025 and Q2 2026.

Commenting on the results, Chip Childs, President and Chief Executive Officer of SkyWest, said, “Our results reflect the value we provide to our customers, including our ongoing opportunities to invest in the fleet. We are well positioned to continue the growth of our E175 fleet in the coming years, enhancing the resiliency of our model for the benefit of our people, our customers and our shareholders. I want to thank our team for their hard work during this busy summer.”

Financial Results

Revenue was $1.12 billion in Q3 2026, up $67 million, or 6%, from $1.05 billion in Q3 2025. SkyWest’s Q3 2026 block hour production increased 5% compared to Q3 2025, driven by more aircraft in scheduled service and strong overall demand. Block hour production in Q3 2026 was also up 1% sequentially from Q2 2026.

1

​

Operating expenses were $941 million in Q3 2026, up $65 million, or 7%, from $876 million in Q3 2025, driven by an expected increase in incremental direct operating costs associated with higher production and an increase in the cost per gallon for prorate fuel in Q3 2026 compared to Q3 2025 and Q2 2026. In Q3 2026, SkyWest revised the estimated useful life of its E175 fleet to 30 years, which reduced depreciation expense in Q3 2026 compared to Q3 2025 and Q2 2026.

Capital and Liquidity

SkyWest had $569 million in cash and marketable securities at September 30, 2026, compared to $707 million at December 31, 2025.

Total debt at September 30, 2026 was $2.4 billion, up from $2.3 billion at June 30, 2026, reflecting $119 million in principal debt payments and $187 million in new debt issued during Q3 2026, and was flat compared to December 31, 2025. Capital expenditures and aircraft deposits during Q3 2026 were $163 million for the purchase of four new E175 aircraft, spare engines, and other fixed assets.

SkyWest repurchased 738,000 shares of its common stock for approximately $75 million during Q3 2026. As of September 30, 2026, SkyWest had approximately $238 million of remaining availability under its existing stock repurchase program.

About SkyWest

SkyWest, Inc. is the holding company for SkyWest Airlines, Inc., SkyWest Charter, LLC (“SWC”) and SkyWest Leasing, Inc., an aircraft equipment leasing company. SkyWest Airlines has a fleet of approximately 500 aircraft connecting passengers to over 240 destinations throughout North America. SkyWest Airlines operates through partnerships with United Airlines, Delta Air Lines, American Airlines, and Alaska Airlines carrying more than 46 million passengers in 2025.

Supplemental Cash Flow Information

​

SkyWest receives certain fixed monthly cash payments under its capacity purchase agreements (“CPAs”) that are attributed to SkyWest’s overhead costs and certain fixed monthly cash payments associated with SkyWest’s aircraft ownership costs. Fixed payments allocated to the non-lease portion are recognized as revenue on a completed block hour basis over the applicable contract term. Fixed payments allocated to the lease portion are accounted for as lease revenue under the CPAs and are recognized on a straight-line basis over the applicable contract term. Fixed monthly cash payments received in excess of revenue recognized during the reporting period are recorded as deferred revenue and revenue recognized in excess of fixed monthly cash payments during the reporting period is recorded as unbilled revenue on SkyWest’s consolidated balance sheet.
