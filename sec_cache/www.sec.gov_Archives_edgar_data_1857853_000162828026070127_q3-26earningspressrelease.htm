TRAEGER ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER FISCAL 2026 RESULTS

Narrows FY26 Revenue and Adjusted EBITDA Guidance Ranges with the Midpoints Unchanged, and Reiterates Gross Margin and Free Cash Flow Guidance

SALT LAKE CITY, UT, November 4, 2026 (BUSINESS WIRE) -- Traeger, Inc. ("Traeger" or the "Company") (NYSE: COOK), creator and category leader of the wood pellet grill, today announced its financial results for the three months ended September 30, 2026.
Third Quarter FY26 Results
•Total revenues decreased 40.4% to $74.7 million
•Grill revenues decreased 48.1% to $39.7 million
•Net loss of $28.5 million
•Adjusted EBITDA loss of $3.3 million
•Operating cash flow of $9.8 million and free cash flow of $8.4 million
Jeremy Andrus, CEO of Traeger, commented, "Our third-quarter revenue came in at the low end of the pacing we outlined last quarter, with some activity shifting into the fourth quarter. We believe consumer demand remains healthier than our reported revenue suggests, particularly at more accessible price points. We expect to return to revenue and Adjusted EBITDA growth in the fourth quarter, supported by channel expansion and the activity that shifted out of the third quarter."
“Looking ahead to our national Lowe’s launch in spring 2027, we believe broader distribution, together with Westwood and Irontop, will expand access to the Traeger brand and create new opportunities for household acquisition," continued Andrus.
“The work we have done through Project Gravity has given us a simpler operating model, a stronger balance sheet, and the capacity to invest behind the consumer and our retail partners. Combined with broader distribution and a stronger product portfolio, we believe these foundations position Traeger to return to profitable growth in 2027," concluded Andrus.

Operating Results for the Third Quarter
Total revenue decreased 40.4% to $74.7 million, compared to $125.4 million in the third quarter last year.
•Grills decreased 48.1% to $39.7 million as compared to the third quarter last year. The decrease reflects comparisons against elevated retailer purchases in the third quarter of last year, as well as product and channel actions under Project Gravity. These dynamics contributed to lower average selling prices and unit volumes compared with the third quarter of last year.
•Consumables decreased 23.1% to $19.4 million as compared to the third quarter last year. The decrease was primarily driven by lower wood pellet sales, reflecting seasonal ordering timing and changes in retail channel dynamics.
•Accessories decreased 34.1% to $15.5 million as compared to the third quarter last year. This decrease was driven primarily by lower sales of MEATER smart thermometers and Traeger branded accessories.
Gross profit decreased to $25.0 million, compared to $48.5 million in the third quarter last year. Gross margin was 33.5% in the third quarter, compared to 38.7% in the same period last year. The decrease in gross margin was driven primarily by the timing of trade spend, deleverage of fixed warehousing expenses on lower revenue, and continued margin pressure in our MEATER business, partially offset by an IEEPA tariff refund benefit.
Sales and marketing expenses were $14.3 million, compared to $20.0 million in the third quarter last year. The decrease in sales and marketing expenses was primarily due to lower employee-related costs and lower demand creation spend, reflecting cost reduction actions associated with Project Gravity.
General and administrative expenses were $19.9 million, compared to $22.2 million in the third quarter last year. The decrease in general and administrative expenses was primarily due to lower employee-related costs, reflecting cost reduction actions associated with Project Gravity.
Restructuring and other costs were $1.7 million, compared to $6.2 million in the third quarter last year. The decrease was primarily driven by reduced consulting fees, as well as lower severance and other personnel costs.
Net loss was $28.5 million in the third quarter, or a loss of $10.33 per diluted share1, compared to net loss of $89.8 million in the third quarter of last year, or a loss of $33.46 per diluted share1, which included a non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $74.7 million.
Adjusted net loss was $18.7 million, or $6.77 per diluted share, compared to $22.3 million, or $8.31 per diluted share in the third quarter last year.2
Adjusted EBITDA was a loss of $3.3 million in the third quarter compared to Adjusted EBITDA of $13.8 million in the same period last year.2

Guidance For Full Year Fiscal 2026
Based on year to date performance and its outlook for the rest of the year, the Company is narrowing the ranges on its total revenue and Adjusted EBITDA guidance and reiterating its gross margin and free cash flow guidance for Fiscal 2026.
•Total revenue is expected to be between $440 million and $460 million, compared to $435 million and $465 million prior
•Gross Margin is expected to be between 40.0% and 41.0%
•Adjusted EBITDA is expected to be between $59 million and $65 million, compared to $57 million and $67 million prior
•Free Cash Flow is expected to be at least $30 million
A reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA and Free Cash Flow guidance to Net Loss and Net cash provided by operating activities on a forward-looking basis cannot be provided without unreasonable efforts, as the Company is unable to provide reconciling information with respect to, in the case of Adjusted EBITDA, adjustments for provision (benefit) for income taxes, interest expense, depreciation and amortization, other (income) expense, stock-based compensation, non-routine legal expenses, wage and hour litigation expenses, goodwill impairment, restructuring and other costs and employee retention tax credits, and, in the case of Free Cash Flow, adjustments for purchases of property, plant, and equipment.

About Traeger
Traeger, headquartered in Salt Lake City, is the creator and category leader of the wood pellet grill, an outdoor cooking system that ignites all-natural hardwoods to grill, smoke, bake, roast, braise, and barbecue. In 2023, Traeger entered the griddle category, further establishing its leadership position in the outdoor cooking space. Traeger grills are versatile and easy to use, empowering cooks of all skill sets to create delicious meals with flavor that cannot be replicated. Grills are at the core of our platform and are complemented by Traeger wood pellets, rubs, sauces, accessories, and MEATER smart thermometers.

RECONCILIATIONS OF AND OTHER INFORMATION REGARDING NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES
(unaudited)
In addition to our results and measures of performance determined in accordance with U.S. GAAP, we believe that certain non-GAAP financial measures are useful in evaluating and comparing our financial and operational performance over multiple periods, identifying trends affecting our business, formulating business plans and making strategic decisions.
Each of Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Net Loss, Adjusted Net Loss per share, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Loss Margin, and Adjusted Gross Margin are key performance measures that our management uses to assess our financial performance and is also used for internal planning and forecasting purposes. We believe that these non-GAAP financial measures are useful to investors and other interested parties in analyzing our financial performance because they provide a comparable overview of our operations across historical periods. In addition, we believe that providing each of Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Net Loss, together with a reconciliation of Net Loss to each such measure, and providing Adjusted Net Loss per share, together with a reconciliation of Net Loss per share to such measure, and Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Loss Margin, and Adjusted Gross Margin, together with a reconciliation of Net Loss Margin and Gross Margin to such measures, helps investors make comparisons between our company and other companies that may have different capital structures, different tax rates, and/or different forms of employee compensation. We also believe that providing Free Cash Flow, together with a reconciliation of Net cash provided by operating activities to such measure, helps investors assess our liquidity and our ability to generate cash from operations. For example, due to finite-lived intangible assets included on our balance sheet following our corporate reorganization in 2017, we have significant non-cash amortization expense attributable to the nature of our capital structure.
Each of Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Net Loss, Adjusted Net Loss per share, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Loss Margin, and Adjusted Gross Margin are used by our management team as an additional measure of our performance for purposes of business decision-making, including managing expenditures, and evaluating potential acquisitions. Period-to-period comparisons of Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Net Loss, and Adjusted Net Loss per share, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Loss Margin, and Adjusted Gross Margin help our management identify additional trends in our financial results that may not be shown solely by period-to-period comparisons of Net Loss or Net Loss per share. Period-to-period comparisons of Free Cash Flow help our management identify additional trends in our liquidity that may not be shown solely by period-to-period comparisons of Net cash provided by operating activities. In addition, we may use Adjusted EBITDA in the incentive compensation programs applicable to some of our employees. Each of Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Net Loss, Adjusted Net Loss per share, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Net Loss Margin, and Adjusted Gross Margin have inherent limitations because of the excluded items, and may not be directly comparable to similarly titled metrics used by other companies.
