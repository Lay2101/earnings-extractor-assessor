IES Holdings Reports Fiscal 2026 Fourth Quarter and Full Year Results; Board Authorizes Additional $350 Million for Share Repurchase Program

HOUSTON — November 20, 2026 — IES Holdings, Inc. (or “IES” or the “Company”) (NASDAQ: IESC) today announced financial results for the quarter and fiscal year ended September 30, 2026.

Fourth Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Revenue of $1.39 billion for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, an increase of 55% compared with $898 million for the same quarter of fiscal 2025
•Operating income of $217.2 million for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, an increase of 108% compared with $104.3 million for the same quarter of fiscal 2025
•Net income attributable to IES of $182.3 million for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, an increase of 79% compared with $101.8 million for the same quarter of fiscal 2025, and diluted earnings per share attributable to common stockholders of $4.50 for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, compared with $2.49 for the same quarter of fiscal 2025
•Adjusted net income attributable to IES (a non-GAAP financial measure, as defined below) of $165.1 million for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, an increase of 114% compared with $77.0 million for the same quarter of fiscal 2025, and diluted adjusted earnings per share attributable to common stockholders of $4.07 for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026, compared with $1.88 for the same quarter of fiscal 2025
•Remaining performance obligations, a GAAP measure of future revenue to be recognized from current contracts with customers, of approximately $3.48 billion as of September 30, 2026
•Backlog (a non-GAAP financial measure, as defined below) of approximately $4.96 billion as of September 30, 2026
•Subsequent to quarter end, on October 5, 2026, completed the acquisition of DBM Global, adding a new Structural line of business and expanded our credit facility from $300 million to $700 million
•Subsequent to quarter end, our Board of Directors authorized an additional $350 million for our share repurchase program

Fiscal Year 2026 Highlights
•Revenue of $4.48 billion for fiscal 2026, an increase of 33% compared with $3.37 billion for fiscal 2025
•Operating income of $605.7 million for fiscal 2026, an increase of 58% compared with $383.5 million for fiscal 2025
•Net income attributable to IES of $536.6 million for fiscal 2026, an increase of 75% compared with $306.0 million for fiscal 2025, and diluted earnings per share attributable to common stockholders of $13.24 for fiscal 2026, compared with $7.51 for fiscal 2025
•Adjusted net income attributable to IES of $459.8 million for fiscal 2026, an increase of 66% compared with $277.8 million for fiscal 2025, and diluted adjusted earnings per share attributable to common stockholders of $11.34 for fiscal 2026 compared with $6.81 for fiscal 2025

Overview of Results
“We are pleased to report outstanding operating performance, resulting in record earnings for fiscal year 2026," said Matt Simmes, President and Chief Executive Officer. "Year-over-year consolidated revenue increased 33% and operating income increased 58%, driven by strong execution by our operating teams, our continued investment in growth, and the strength of the key markets in which we operate. I also want to recognize our teams for their focus on continuous improvement in safety over the year, reinforcing our commitment to a strong safety culture across the organization. With record levels of backlog at September 30, 2026, we expect continued strong organic growth in fiscal 2027 in our Communications, Commercial & Industrial and Infrastructure Solutions segments.

"In October 2026 we completed our acquisition of DBM Global, establishing a new Structural line of business. We are excited about the addition of DBM Global's engineering, fabrication and erection capabilities and approximately 4,000 skilled employees to the IES team. The addition of DBM Global broadens the range of products and services we offer our customers, and we are optimistic about the new business opportunities that its skilled workforce and over two million square feet of operating

2




facilities will enable in fiscal 2027. This acquisition also added backlog of $2.6 billion, which is not included in our September 30, 2026 backlog amounts described above.”

Our Communications segment’s revenue was $1.69 billion in fiscal 2026, an increase of 48% compared with fiscal 2025, driven by investments we have made in the business to keep pace with the continuously increasing demand from our customers in the data center end market. The segment's operating income increased to $290.0 million for fiscal 2026, compared with $166.5 million for fiscal 2025, as we benefited from strong project execution, our ability to scale effectively, and strong market conditions.

Our Commercial & Industrial segment’s revenue was $799.5 million in fiscal 2026, an increase of 83% compared with fiscal 2025. The increase in revenue was primarily driven by continued strong demand and successful execution in the data center end market, as we benefited from our efforts to expand our capabilities and trained workforce in that space. Segment operating income for fiscal 2026 was $163.1 million, compared with $47.3 million for fiscal 2025. Our operating margins, particularly in the second half of fiscal 2026, benefited from some large, quick-turning projects we executed at favorable margins, and our teams rose to the challenge of executing these projects successfully.
Our Infrastructure Solutions segment’s revenue was $826.6 million in fiscal 2026, an increase of 66% compared with fiscal 2025, driven by continued strong demand in our custom engineered solutions business, primarily for the data center end market, where investments we have made in capacity over the past several years have enabled us to meet customer demand. Operating income for fiscal 2026 was $194.4 million, compared with $118.5 million for fiscal 2025. The year-over-year profit improvement was driven by higher volumes and improved pricing. Operating income as a percent of revenue decreased year over year, as we continue to invest in our more recent acquisitions, which typically come on line at a lower margin than more mature facilities. However, this impact was largely mitigated by our ability to efficiently scale fixed costs as we grow. Gulf Island, which we acquired in January 2026, contributed $151 million of revenue and $11 million of operating income for the fiscal year.

Our Residential segment’s revenue was $1.22 billion in fiscal 2026, a decrease of 7% compared with fiscal 2025, reflecting weak demand in the housing market, which continues to be affected by housing affordability challenges, elevated mortgage rates, availability and cost of insurance, and overall economic uncertainty. However, the impact of these factors was partly offset by continued expansion of our plumbing and HVAC trades into new geographic markets. As the cost of inputs rose throughout fiscal 2026, weak demand limited our ability to recover cost increases through pricing actions in our single-family business. In our multi-family business, lower revenue year-over-year reflects the impacts of prolonged elevated borrowing costs for project owners, which resulted in lower backlog entering fiscal 2026. As a result of these factors, the Residential segment’s operating income was $40.1 million for fiscal 2026, compared with $103.8 million for fiscal 2025.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures and Other Adjustments
This press release includes adjusted net income attributable to IES, adjusted diluted earnings per share attributable to common stockholders, and backlog, and, in the non-GAAP reconciliation tables included herein, adjusted net income attributable to common stockholders, EBITDA, adjusted EBITDA and adjusted income from operations before income taxes, each of which is a financial measure not calculated in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the U.S. (“GAAP”). Management believes that these measures provide useful information to our investors by, in the case of adjusted net income attributable to IES, adjusted net income attributable to common stockholders, adjusted earnings per share attributable to common stockholders, adjusted EBITDA and adjusted income from operations before income taxes, distinguishing certain nonrecurring events such as litigation settlements, significant expenses associated with leadership changes, or gains or losses from the sale of a business, noncash events, such as impairment charges, items that are not indicative of quarterly business performance such as gains and losses on our investments, or, in the case of backlog, providing a common measurement used in IES's industry, as described further below, and that these measures, when reconciled to the most directly comparable GAAP measures, help our investors to better identify underlying trends in the operations of our business and facilitate easier comparisons of our financial performance with prior and future periods and to our peers. Non-GAAP financial

5




measures should not be considered in isolation from, or as a substitute for, financial information calculated in accordance with GAAP. Investors are encouraged to review the reconciliation of these non-GAAP measures to their most directly comparable GAAP financial measures, which has been provided in the financial tables included in this press release.
Remaining performance obligations represent the unrecognized revenue value of our contract commitments. While backlog is not a defined term under GAAP, it is a common measurement used in IES’s industry and IES believes this non-GAAP measure enables it to more effectively forecast its future results and better identify future operating trends that may not otherwise be apparent. IES’s remaining performance obligations are a component of IES’s backlog calculation, which also includes signed agreements and letters of intent which we do not have a legal right to enforce prior to work starting. These arrangements are excluded from remaining performance obligations until work begins. IES’s methodology for determining backlog may not be comparable to the methodologies used by other companies.

About IES Holdings, Inc.

IES designs and installs integrated electrical and technology systems, provides infrastructure solutions and services, and delivers structural steel engineering, fabrication and erection to a variety of end markets, including data centers, residential housing, and commercial, industrial and infrastructure facilities. Our more than 16,000 employees serve clients primarily in the United States. For more information about IES, please visit www.ies-co.com.
