Ondas Posts Record Q3 2026 Revenue of $167.5 Million on Strong Bookings and Backlog Growth; Raises Full-Year Outlook

 

Record Q3 revenue of $167.5 million, up 100% sequentially and more than 16-fold year over year; pro forma organic revenue grew 64% year-over-year

 

Captured $214 million in new orders during Q3; backlog increased 36% sequentially to $835 million

 

Raises full-year 2026 revenue target to at least $550 million; targets more than $250 million of Q4 revenue and at least 75% pro forma organic year-over-year growth in Q4

 

Initiates 2027 revenue target of at least $1.1 billion, excluding additional acquisitions

 

Expects operating-platform Adjusted EBITDA profitability in Q4 2026 and pulls forward company-wide Adjusted EBITDA profitability target to Q3 2027

 

Expanded U.S. defense, sovereign AI, manufacturing and supply-chain capabilities through Ondas Sentinel, Regna AI and targeted acquisitions

 

Conference call scheduled for today, November 12 at 8:30 a.m. ET

 

WEST PALM BEACH, FL / November 12, 2026 / Ondas Inc. (NASDAQ: ONDS) (“Ondas” or the “Company”), a leading provider of advanced autonomous systems and next-generation defense and security technologies and services, reported record financial and operating results for the third quarter of 2026. Ondas generated revenue of $167.5 million in the third quarter, compared with $83.8 million in the second quarter of 2026 and $10.1 million in the third quarter of 2025. The results represent approximately 100% sequential revenue growth and more than a sixteen-fold year-over-year increase.

 

“This quarter demonstrates the growing benefits of Ondas’ integrated operating platform,” said Eric Brock, Chairman and CEO of Ondas. “Our businesses are gaining access to larger customers, broader channels, and stronger production and field-support capabilities, allowing mission-ready technologies to move more quickly from evaluation into deployment. The next phase is about disciplined execution: delivering on customer commitments, integrating our capabilities, and converting demand to sustainable growth and improving profitability.”

 

“Our differentiation increasingly comes from combining autonomous systems, mission software, AI, and critical components into solutions that can be deployed and supported across multiple domains. Ondas Sentinel strengthens our ability to pursue and execute complex U.S. defense programs, while Regna AI extends our sovereign AI capabilities and creates opportunities to serve customers beyond our own hardware platforms. Together, these initiatives expand our addressable market, strengthen our customer relationships, and increase the software, services, and repeat revenue potential of the Ondas platform.

 

“We expect a strong finish to 2026, with momentum accelerating in the fourth quarter as volume deliveries ramp across existing programs and we convert pipeline opportunities into customer programs. Our order book remains healthy, our pipeline continues to expand, and our ability to produce, deliver, and sustain solutions for customers continues to strengthen. We are investing to support visible customer demand and build the production, supply-chain, deployment, and sustainment infrastructure required to deliver at scale. Over the next twelve months and beyond, we expect this growth to support meaningful operating leverage and improved profitability, supporting continued investment in innovation, market expansion, and long-term shareholder value.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Revenue increased 100% sequentially to $167.5 million in Q3 2026, compared to $83.8 million in Q2 2026, while representing a more than 16-fold increase from $10.1 million in Q3 2025. On a pro forma organic basis, revenue increased 64% year over year, assuming the businesses owned and operated during Q3 2026 were also owned and operated in Q3 2025. Ondas believes that strong organic growth reflects the benefits of platform scale, shared technology, and expanded customer access. The increase further reflects strong performance across the Company's portfolio, particularly C-UAS systems, where demand remains strong given the long-term need to protect the lower skies across civilian and military airspace, as well as in precision strike and land systems.

 

Gross profit was $36.5 million in Q3 2026, as compared to $36.1 million in Q2 2026 and $2.6 million in Q3 2025. Gross margin was 21.8% in Q3 2026, as compared to 43.1% in Q2 2026 and 25.8% in Q3 2025. The sequential decline in gross margin was related to higher amortization of capitalized intellectual property, the step-up in acquired inventory associated with purchase accounting, as well as inefficiencies in the early stages of ramping large programs. Adjusted Gross Profit and Adjusted Gross Margin were $58.3 million and 34.8%, respectively, in Q3 2026, as compared to Adjusted Gross Profit and Adjusted Gross Margin of $42.3 million and 50.4%, respectively, in Q2 2026. The increase in Adjusted Gross Profit reflects higher revenue, from organic growth as well as from contributions of businesses acquired during the period. The lower Adjusted Gross Margin percentage reflects production and delivery inefficiencies, particularly in the precision strike segment, which represented a disproportionate share of revenue during the period. The Company expects similar gross margin levels in Q4 2026 due to mix with a recovery to historical levels with normal quarterly variability.

 

Operating expenses increased to $226.0 million in Q3 2026, compared with $199.1 million in Q2 2026 and $18.1 million in Q3 2025. The increase was primarily driven by $88.1 million of non-cash expenses during the quarter, mainly comprising $57.8 million of stock-based compensation, and $25.3 million of amortization expense, together with $14.1 million of acquisition-related cash expenses. Acquisition-related expenses were elevated due to the timing of several acquisitions, including DZYNE, which was Ondas’ largest acquisition to date.

 

Adjusted Cash Operating Expenses were $118.7 million in Q3 2026, compared to $88.8 million in Q2 2026 and $10.9 million in Q3 2025. This level of operating expense was consistent with our expectations and reflected the inclusion of acquired businesses during the quarter. The growth in adjusted cash operating expenses reflected the inclusion of newly acquired businesses along with continued investment in Ondas' operating platform and infrastructure in support of our expected significant revenue acceleration in the fourth quarter of 2026 and beyond. Research and development expense increased to $61.6 million in Q3 2026, up $30 million sequentially, reflecting the Company’s rapid expansion of internal development capabilities, a significantly accelerated pace of product innovation, and intensified integration efforts across its growing technology platform.

 

Operating loss increased to $189.5 million in Q3 2026, compared to a $162.9 million loss in Q2 2026, and a $15.5 million loss in Q3 2025. The increase from both periods was the result of the changes described above and includes the aforementioned large non-cash expenses.

 

Total other income, net was $133.1 million in Q3 2026, compared to other income of $44.2 million in Q2 2026 and other income of $8.3 million in Q3 2025. Other income includes non-cash gains of $125.1 million relating to warrants issued in connection with the October 2025 and January 2026 equity raises. Because these warrants are remeasured at fair value each reporting period, the resulting non-cash gains and losses can create significant volatility in reported earnings that are unrelated to the Company's core operating performance, cash flows, or the economic terms of the warrants.

 

Net loss was $64.7 million in Q3 2026, which included the non-cash items mentioned above, as compared to net loss of $89.7 million in Q2 2026, and a net loss of $7.5 million in Q3 2025.

 

Adjusted EBITDA loss was $59.7 million in Q3 2026, as compared to a loss of $46.2 million in Q2 2026 and a loss of $8.1 million in Q3 2025. The higher sequential loss reflects the lower sequential gross margin; the investments made in continuing to scale Ondas' operating platform and corporate development activities to support the expected significant revenue expansion in Q4 2026 and into 2027.

Operational and Financial Outlook

 

The Company expects continued strong momentum and is now targeting at least $550 million in full year revenue for 2026, which is an increase from the prior target of between $525 – $550 million. For Q4 2026, the Company is targeting revenue of greater than $250 million. The new full year target represents a projected year-on-year growth of greater than 10x reported 2025 results. Growth continues to be supported by both organic growth in the core business and the addition of acquired businesses. Ondas estimates pro forma year-over-year organic growth will be at least 75% for the fourth quarter of 2026.

 

Ondas is issuing an initial target of at least $1.1 billion in revenue for the full year 2027, which would represent 100% year over year growth and more than 40% year over year organic growth. The Company expects backlog to grow sequentially in the fourth quarter of 2026 based upon strong order visibility.

 

Growth in the fourth quarter is expected to be broad-based across Ondas' product portfolio, supported by a strong pipeline and existing backlog. Revenue generation is also expected to be supported by specific customer orders and programs already in backlog as well as production and delivery ramps in key products such as ULTRA and IonStrike, which have significant upside potential in 2027 and beyond as Ondas pursues program opportunities globally. Ondas expects continued volume shipments related to orders captured by Mistral under the $982 million Lethal Unmanned Strike (LUS) IDIQ award with the U.S. Army. Ondas also expects to begin volume deliveries in the fourth quarter for the $140 million combat engineering vehicles program announced earlier in the year.

 

The elevated losses in the first three quarters of 2026 represented a front-loading of expenses ahead of the continued significant revenue ramp into 2027, as well as some inefficiencies in scaling some large programs in the third quarter. The Company believes the early ramp-up inefficiencies that negatively affected third-quarter gross margin were temporary and that the elevated operating expenses in 2026 represented investments necessary to support long-term growth. The Company expects Adjusted EBITDA losses to show significant declines in the coming quarters through higher operating leverage benefiting from strong growth in revenues and gross profits driven by visible demand tailwinds and increased operating leverage from Ondas expanding platform.

 

The Company continues to expect to see Adjusted EBITDA profitability in Q4 2026 at the operating platform level, which includes OAS and Ondas Sentinel. Ondas now expects company-wide Adjusted EBITDA profitability by Q3 2027, one quarter earlier than its prior expectations driven by revenue and gross profit generation relative to moderating operating expense growth.

 

Ondas maintains a strong balance sheet and liquidity profile and held approximately $820 million in cash, restricted cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments as of September 30, 2026.

About Ondas Inc.

 

Ondas Inc. (Nasdaq: ONDS) is a leading provider of autonomous systems, robotics, and mission-critical technologies for defense, homeland security, public safety, critical infrastructure, and industrial markets. The Company develops and deploys integrated unmanned and autonomous platforms across air, ground, and stratospheric environments, including autonomous drone systems, counter-UAS technologies, robotic ground systems, advanced unmanned aircraft and propulsion solutions, demining and engineering systems, and integrated sensing and communications technologies designed to support intelligence, surveillance, reconnaissance, security, and operational missions in complex environments. Non-GAAP Measures

As required by the rules of the Securities and Exchange Commission ("SEC"), we provide a reconciliation of our non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable GAAP measures. These reconciliations are set forth in the tables below.

We believe that Adjusted earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation, and amortization ("Adjusted EBITDA") is a useful supplemental measure for evaluating our operating performance and period to period trends because it eliminates the impact of items that primarily reflect our capital structure, tax position, non-cash accounting charges, acquisition-related transaction costs, and other items that management does not consider indicative of ongoing operating performance. Adjusted EBITDA should be considered in addition to, and not as a substitute for, net income (loss) and other measures prepared in accordance with GAAP. Adjusted EBITDA removes the effects of interest and financing-related items, depreciation and amortization, income taxes, stock-based compensation and expense, acquisition-related expenses, change in fair value of contingent consideration and other acquisition related obligations, acquisition related inventory fair value step ups, qualifying acquisition related employee costs, qualifying acquisition integration costs and other non-operating gains and losses. Management believes that excluding these items enhances comparability across periods and facilitates analysis of underlying operating trends.

Adjusted Cash Operating Expense is a non-GAAP financial measure that represents total operating expenses excluding depreciation, amortization of intangible assets, acquisition-related expenses, change in fair value of contingent consideration and other acquisition related obligations, qualifying acquisition related employee costs, qualifying acquisition integration costs and stock-based compensation and expense. The most directly comparable GAAP measure to Adjusted Cash Operating Expense is total operating expenses. Management believes Adjusted Cash Operating Expense provides useful supplemental information by facilitating analysis of the Company’s ongoing cash operating costs and meaningful period-to-period comparisons. Management uses this measure for internal cost management, budgeting, and to evaluate operating trends excluding specified non-cash accounting charges and certain acquisition related cash costs. Adjusted Cash Operating Expense should be considered in addition to, and not as a substitute for, total operating expenses prepared in accordance with GAAP.

Beginning in the period ended June 30, 2026, the Company revised its calculation of Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted Cash Operating Expense to exclude changes in the fair value of contingent consideration and other acquisition related obligations. These amounts reflect periodic remeasurement adjustments required under U.S. GAAP and are primarily driven by changes in estimates and assumptions related to future earn-out payments. Management believes excluding these acquisition-related fair value adjustments improves period-to-period comparability and provides investors with additional insight into the Company's operating performance. This revision did not affect any previously reported Adjusted EBITDA or Adjusted Cash Operating Expense amounts because no gains or losses related to changes in the fair value of contingent consideration were recognized in the prior periods presented. In connection with this change, the Company renamed 'Cash Operating Expense' to 'Adjusted Cash Operating Expense'. The revised caption is intended to more clearly communicate the measure as a management-defined non-GAAP performance measure that excludes specified cash and noncash expenses and does not represent all operating expenses requiring cash settlement.

Also beginning in the period ended June 30, 2026, the Company introduced Adjusted Gross Profit and Adjusted Gross Margin. Adjusted Gross Profit is a non-GAAP financial measure that represents gross profit excluding amortization of acquisition-related intangible assets, acquisition related inventory fair value step ups and stock-based compensation and expense included in cost of goods sold. Adjusted Gross Margin is a non-GAAP financial measure that represents Adjusted Gross Profit as a percentage of revenue. The most directly comparable GAAP measures to Adjusted Gross Profit and Adjusted Gross Margin are gross profit and gross margin (gross profit as a percentage of revenue), respectively. Management believes these measures provide investors with additional insight into the underlying profitability of the Company's products and services, operating performance and period-to-period trends. Comparative prior-period amounts have been presented on a consistent basis.

Beginning with the period ended September 30, 2026, the Company further revised the definition of Adjusted EBITDA to exclude acquisition related inventory fair value step ups, qualifying acquisition related employee costs and qualifying acquisition integration costs. Correspondingly, the definition of Adjusted Cash Operating Expense was revised to exclude qualifying acquisition related employee costs and qualifying acquisition integration costs. In addition, the definition of Adjusted Gross Profit was revised to exclude acquisition related inventory fair value step ups. Management believes these revisions facilitate period to period comparability and analysis of the Company’s underlying operating performance and cost trends. These revisions did not affect previously reported non-GAAP measures, as no amounts qualifying under the newly added categories were recognized in prior periods presented.

Management uses Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Cash Operating Expense, Adjusted Gross Profit, and Adjusted Gross Margin together with GAAP results, in making operating and planning decisions and in evaluating the Company's ongoing performance. Other companies may calculate similarly titled non-GAAP measures differently, and therefore our non-GAAP measures may not be comparable to measures used by other companies.
