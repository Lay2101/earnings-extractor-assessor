YETI Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results
Net Sales Increase 4% and Adjusted Net Sales Increase 6%
EPS Increases 25% and Adjusted EPS Increases 49%
Updates 2026 Outlook

Austin, Texas, November 12, 2026 – YETI Holdings, Inc. (“YETI”) (NYSE: YETI) today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended October 3, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
•Sales increased 4% and Adjusted sales increased 6%, led by 8% Drinkware growth and 19% international growth
•Gross margin increased 180 basis points and Adjusted gross margin increased 310 basis points
•Operating income increased 12% to $61.0 million, or 12.0% of sales and Adjusted operating income increased 35% to $90.0 million, or 17.4% of adjusted sales
•EPS increased 25% to $0.60 and Adjusted EPS increased 49% to $0.91
•Repurchased approximately 0.5 million shares for $20 million

Updated 2026 Outlook
•Adjusted sales growth of approximately 6% compared to 7% to 8% previously
•Adjusted operating income margin of approximately 14.9%, unchanged compared to prior outlook
•Adjusted EPS of $2.90 to $2.95, reflecting 16% to 18% growth, compared to $2.94 to $3.00 previously

Matt Reintjes, Chair of the Board, President, and Chief Executive Officer, commented, “YETI delivered Q3 adjusted sales growth of more than 6%, reflecting the increasing breadth and balance of our growing global business, with Drinkware up 8% and international up 19%. Strong operating discipline drove margin expansion, with profits growing significantly faster than sales. Our continued strong free cash flow generation supported our long-term growth plans while also returning capital through share repurchases.

This performance demonstrates continued progress against our Investor Day “Built for the Wild” strategy. It reflects the dedication of our teams and partners, and the multi-year investments we have made across brand, innovation, and global capabilities. Together, these results reinforce the strength of the financial model we outlined. While demand was uneven at times during the quarter, particularly in the US and Canadian markets, the expanding diversity of our products, channels, and geographies strongly supported our overall performance. Looking ahead, we anticipate the consumer environment will remain cautious and put pressure on demand in Q4. Although we are mindful of these near-term dynamics, the strength of our strategy, the durability of our brand, our innovation cadence, and our business model position us well to navigate the short-term market, create value and continue building for the long term.”

Mr. Reintjes continued, “Across our product portfolio, we are strengthening the iconic product platforms that built YETI while also driving our portfolio expansion as you have seen in the past few weeks. At the same time, our brand continues to grow in reach and relevance through authentic storytelling, partnerships, and community engagement around the world. For both product and brand, international remains an especially compelling opportunity. We are seeing continued momentum across Europe and Asia with our first brand stores opening in Japan and Korea and our initial marketplace launch in China in the past few weeks. As we scale, we are also strengthening the capabilities behind the business, across innovation, supply chain, customization, digital, and go-to-market execution.”

Third Quarter 2026 Results

Sales increased 4% to $509.4 million, reflecting broad-based performance across categories and regions.

Adjusted sales, which exclude the current period impact from adjustments to the 2023 product recall reserves, increased 6% to $517.9 million.

Sales by Channel
•Wholesale channel sales increased 19% to $237.3 million, driven by robust performance across the US, Europe and Australia.
1


•Direct-to-consumer (“DTC”) channel sales decreased 6% to $272.2 million. DTC channel adjusted sales decreased 3% to $280.7 million primarily due to declines in US owned e-commerce and the US Amazon Marketplace, partially offset by strong growth across several key international markets, growth in Corporate Sales, including in the US.
Sales by Category
•Drinkware sales increased 8% to $284.0 million, driven by international and US growth, supported by continued innovation and strength in our core product portfolio.
•Coolers & Equipment sales were flat at $215.6 million. Coolers & Equipment adjusted sales increased 4% to $224.1 million, reflecting continued strength across core and expanded categories, including cases & storage, hard coolers, and bags, supported by ongoing product innovation.
Sales by Region
•International sales increased 19% to $119.1 million, reflecting strong growth in Europe and Australia, as well as continued expansion in Japan.
•US sales increased 1% to $390.3 million. US adjusted sales increased 3% to $398.8 million, primarily driven by growth across Coolers & Equipment as well as Drinkware growth.

Gross profit increased 8% to $293.8 million. Gross margin increased 180 basis points to 57.7%. Gross margin expansion included a 200 basis point favorable impact from lower net tariff costs and a 110 basis point benefit from operational drivers. Operational drivers included continued pricing discipline and lower inbound freight, partially offset by higher product costs driven by commodity inflation. These benefits were partially offset by a 130 basis point impact from the product recall reserves.

Adjusted gross profit increased 12% to $305.5 million. Adjusted gross margin increased 310 basis points to 59.0%. Adjusted gross margin expansion included a 200 basis point favorable impact from lower net tariff costs and a 110 basis point benefit from operational drivers. Operational drivers included continued pricing discipline and lower inbound freight, partially offset by higher product costs driven by commodity inflation.

Selling, general, and administrative (“SG&A”) expenses increased 7% to $232.8 million. As a percentage of sales, SG&A expenses increased 100 basis points to 45.7%. SG&A expenses increased primarily due to the product recall reserves, higher distribution and fulfillment costs, and continued growth investments.

Adjusted SG&A expenses increased 5% to $215.5 million. As a percentage of adjusted sales, adjusted SG&A expenses decreased 60 basis points to 41.6%. This improvement was primarily driven by operating expense leverage on higher sales, particularly across distribution and fulfillment costs and marketing expenses, partially offset by continued growth investments.

Operating income increased 12% to $61.0 million, or 12.0% of sales.

Adjusted operating income increased 35% to $90.0 million, or 17.4% of adjusted sales.

Net income increased 13% to $44.4 million, or 8.7% of sales. Net income per diluted share increased 25% to $0.60.

Adjusted net income increased 35% to $67.2 million, or 13.0% of adjusted sales. Adjusted net income per diluted share increased 49% to $0.91.

Balance Sheet and Liquidity Review

We continue to maintain strong liquidity with cash exceeding total debt, excluding finance leases and unamortized deferred financing fees, and an undrawn revolving credit facility of $300 million as of October 3, 2026.

Inventory increased 22% to $394.2 million. The increase reflects the comparison to the prior year’s lower inventory levels, which were driven by supply constraints, as well as the buildup to support fourth quarter demand and 2027 growth.

Capital Allocation Update

We continue to expect strong free cash flow generation and remain committed to investing in our business to drive sustainable growth and enhance long-term stockholder value, including through share repurchases.

Pursuant to our existing $500 million share repurchase authorization, in the third quarter of 2026, we repurchased approximately 0.5 million shares for $20.0 million, bringing our total share repurchases in 2026 to approximately $150 million. As of October 3, 2026, approximately $350 million remained available for repurchases under our share repurchase program.

Fiscal 2026 Outlook

Mr. Reintjes concluded, “While consumers appear to be increasingly selective around discretionary purchases, the performance we are seeing across several key areas of the business remains encouraging, including the reception to the innovation underpinning our Drinkware category. Although we are taking a more cautious view of demand through the balance of the year, we are staying on offense as it relates to brand, innovation and our evolving go-to-market to meet the unmet demand while also driving increased profitability. With our strong financial foundation and disciplined approach to capital allocation, we are uniquely well positioned to deliver sustained growth, attractive profitability, and meaningful long-term value for our shareholders.

We continue to expect a strong year, with approximately 6% adjusted sales growth and adjusted EPS growth of 16% to 18%. We also expect $200 million to $225 million of free cash flow, underscoring the strength of our financial model and providing flexibility to invest in the business while returning capital to shareholders.”

For Fiscal 2026 compared to Fiscal 2025, YETI updated its Fiscal 2026 Outlook.

•Adjusted sales to increase approximately 6% (versus previous outlook of 7% to 8%), reflecting a more cautious consumer environment through the balance of the year;
•Adjusted operating income to increase 7% to 9% (versus previous outlook of 10% to 12%), with adjusted operating income as a percentage of adjusted sales of approximately 14.9% (consistent with previous outlook);
•An effective tax rate of approximately 24% (consistent with previous outlook);
•Adjusted net income per diluted share between $2.90 and $2.95, representing growth of approximately 16% to 18% (versus previous outlook of $2.94 to $3.00);
•Diluted weighted average shares outstanding of approximately 75.1 million (versus previous outlook of 75.4 million);
•Capital expenditures of approximately $60 million to $70 million (consistent with previous outlook); and
•Free cash flow of approximately $200 million to $225 million (consistent with previous outlook).

About YETI Holdings, Inc.
Headquartered in Austin, Texas, YETI is a global designer, retailer, and distributor of innovative outdoor products. From coolers and drinkware to bags and apparel, YETI products are built to meet the unique and varying needs of diverse outdoor pursuits, whether in the remote wilderness, at the beach, or anywhere life takes you. By consistently delivering high-performing, exceptional products, we have built a strong following of brand loyalists throughout the world, ranging from serious outdoor enthusiasts to individuals who simply value products of uncompromising quality and design. We have an unwavering commitment to outdoor and recreation communities, and we are relentless in our pursuit of building superior products for people to confidently enjoy life outdoors and beyond. For more information, please visit www.YETI.com.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
Beginning in the third quarter of 2026, product recall reserve adjustments related to our 2023 voluntary recalls are excluded from non-GAAP financial measures. This represents a change from the approach we adopted with our fourth quarter of 2025 results, under which we did not exclude product recall reserve adjustments from our non-GAAP results, but is consistent with the treatment we applied from fiscal 2022 through fiscal 2024. We believe this presentation better reflects our underlying operating performance and improves comparability across periods. Our non-GAAP results for the nine months ended October 3, 2026 also exclude product recall reserve adjustments recorded in the first and second quarters of 2026, which were included in our previously reported non-GAAP results for those periods. Non-GAAP results for the fourth quarter and fiscal year 2025 and the first and second quarters of 2026, recast to conform to this presentation, are available in the document posted as Supplemental Information under Q3 2026 on our investor relations website at investors.yeti.com.

In addition to our results determined in accordance with GAAP, we supplement our results with non-GAAP financial measures, including adjusted net sales, adjusted gross profit, adjusted gross margin, adjusted SG&A expenses, adjusted operating income, adjusted net income, adjusted net income per diluted share (which we also refer to as adjusted EPS), free cash flow as well as adjusted gross profit, adjusted SG&A expenses, adjusted operating income and adjusted net income as a percentage of adjusted net sales.

Our management uses these non-GAAP financial measures in conjunction with GAAP financial measures to measure our profitability and to evaluate our financial performance. We believe that these non-GAAP financial measures provide meaningful supplemental information regarding the underlying operating performance of our business and are appropriate to enhance an overall understanding of our financial performance. These non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as analytical tools in that they do not reflect all of the amounts associated with our results of operations as determined in accordance with GAAP. Because of these limitations, these non-GAAP financial measures should be considered along with GAAP financial performance measures. The presentation of these non-GAAP financial measures is not intended to be considered in isolation or as a substitute for, or superior to, financial information prepared and presented in accordance with GAAP.
