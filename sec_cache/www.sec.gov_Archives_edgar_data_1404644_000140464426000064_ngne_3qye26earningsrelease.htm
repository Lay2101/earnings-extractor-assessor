Neurogene Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Highlights Recent Updates

Topline data from Embolden™ registrational trial anticipated in 2H 2027

Presented new preclinical data highlighting the purposeful design of NGN-401 for Rett syndrome, including rapid onset and durable expression of full-length MECP2 transgene with single-stranded AAV construct

NGN-401 at the 1E15 vg dose remains generally well-tolerated (N=35) as of data cutoff date of November 8, 2026

On track to complete PPQ runs by year-end 2026

Strong cash position provides runway into first quarter of 2029

NEW YORK – Nov. 9, 2026 – Neurogene Inc. (Nasdaq: NGNE), a clinical-stage company founded to bring life-changing genetic medicines to patients and families affected by rare neurological diseases, today announced third quarter 2026 financial results and highlighted recent corporate updates.

“During the third quarter, we continued to execute across all aspects of our NGN-401 program as we advance toward potential commercialization,” said Rachel McMinn, Ph.D., Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Neurogene. “With topline data from the Embolden registrational trial expected in the second half of 2027, we are progressing key BLA and commercial-readiness activities, including PPQ runs that we believe remain on track for completion this year. We are also continuing to strengthen our engagement with the Rett syndrome community and the broader medical community to increase awareness and understanding of NGN-401 and the growing body of data supporting its potential as a best-in-class, one-time treatment for Rett syndrome. Together, these efforts are aimed at positioning us for a successful launch, if approved.”

Third Quarter 2026 and Recent Highlights

NGN-401 Gene Therapy for the Treatment of Rett Syndrome

•Anticipate reporting topline data from the Embolden™ registrational trial in the second half of 2027
oAs previously announced, the Embolden trial was overenrolled due to strong demand from the Rett syndrome community. Neurogene elected to include all 25 participants dosed in the primary efficacy analysis. The update to the statistical analysis plan was aligned with the FDA through the START Pilot Program. The



corresponding threshold for success to reject the null hypothesis is 9 of 25 participants (36%). All other previously aligned key elements of the trial design remain unchanged.
•Presented an encore of the positive long-term Phase 1/2 data, as of the data cutoff date of June 16, 2026, at the 55th Child Neurology Society Annual Meeting
oPhase 1/2 data demonstrated developmental milestones were gained in a progressive, stepwise sequence, suggesting a restart of development post-treatment
•Presented data at the European Society of Gene and Cell Therapy 2026 Annual Congress that highlighted the key differentiating design elements of NGN-401 for Rett syndrome:
oNew preclinical data showed rapid onset of action and durable expression of MeCP2 protein following single-stranded AAV (ssAAV) delivery of the NGN-401 construct; the data provided additional evidence that self-complementary AAV does not offer a meaningful advantage in onset of action in the treatment of Rett syndrome
NGN-401 was designed to use ssAAV to accommodate the fully functional, full-length MECP2 transgene rather than a minigene
oNew in vivo evaluation of transgene regulation technologies demonstrated that miRNA-based auto-regulatory technology was not responsive to endogenous MeCP2 levels on a cell-by-cell basis; the data showed expression in both MeCP2-null and MeCP2-expressing neurons
oAn encore presentation supported the therapeutic rationale for the intracerebroventricular (ICV) administration of central nervous system (CNS)-targeted gene therapy, including NGN-401; ICV administration is a standard neurosurgical procedure performed thousands of times per year, and preclinical data demonstrated broader CNS biodistribution to brain regions central to Rett syndrome pathophysiology, as compared with intrathecal lumbar administration
•Partnered with the International Rett Syndrome Foundation to host a webinar focused on educating the Rett syndrome community about NGN-401
•NGN-401 at the 1E15 vg dose has been generally well-tolerated in the Phase 1/2 trial and Embolden (N=35) as of November 8, 2026
•On track to complete Process Performance Qualification (PPQ) runs by the end of 2026 to support the planned Biologics License Application (BLA) submission

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

•Cash, Cash Equivalents and Short-Term Investments: Cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments as of September 30, 2026 were $359.7 million and are expected to fund planned operations into the first quarter of 2029.
•Research & Development (R&D) Expenses: R&D expenses were $23.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $17.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in R&D expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was primarily driven by higher costs associated with the development of NGN-401, including Rett syndrome clinical trial activities and chemistry, manufacturing and controls (CMC) activities supporting the NGN-401 program. The increase also reflected higher employee-related expenses due to an increase in R&D headcount and increased non-cash stock-based compensation expenses. The increase was partially offset by lower spending on the CLN5 Batten disease program.
•General & Administrative (G&A) Expenses: G&A expenses were $8.9 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $6.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in G&A expenses was primarily driven by higher non-cash stock-based compensation expense, driven by an increase in headcount as well as an increase in performance-based stock unit awards following achievement of specified corporate milestones. Additional increases were driven by corporate and pre-commercial activities, higher employee-related expenses and professional fees.
•Net Loss: Net loss was $29.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to $21.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

About Neurogene
Neurogene (Nasdaq: NGNE) is a clinical-stage biotechnology company focused on developing life-changing genetic medicines for people and their families impacted by devastating neurological diseases. The Company is using a biology-first approach paired with optimized delivery to develop purpose-built genetic medicines, including programs powered by its novel and proprietary EXACT™ transgene regulation technology. Neurogene is advancing its lead gene therapy program, NGN-401, as a potential best-in-class, one-time treatment for Rett syndrome. For more information, visit neurogene.com or follow on LinkedIn.

About NGN-401
NGN-401 is an investigational AAV9 gene therapy in late-stage clinical development as a potential best-in-class, one-time treatment for Rett syndrome. It is the only clinical candidate to deliver the full-length human MECP2 gene and includes Neurogene’s EXACT™ transgene regulation technology, which is designed to deliver consistent, tightly controlled MeCP2 protein expression on a cell-by-cell basis. NGN-401 is delivered through intracerebroventricular administration to achieve the broadest targeting directly to the brain and nervous system based on nonclinical biodistribution data. NGN-401 is being evaluated in the Embolden™



registrational clinical trial. Data from the Phase 1/2 trial (as of June 16, 2026) have shown that participants experienced multidomain, durable gains with continued developmental milestone acquisition observed over time, and NGN-401 at the 1E15 vg dose has been generally well-tolerated. NGN-401 has received Breakthrough Therapy, Regenerative Medicine Advanced Therapy, Fast Track, Orphan Drug and Rare Pediatric Disease designations and selection for the START Pilot Program from the U.S. Food and Drug Administration, Advanced Therapy Medicinal Product, Orphan and Priority Medicines designations from the European Medicines Agency and Innovative Licensing and Application Pathway designation from the United Kingdom Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency.
